Wonder of the world

The bridge is no longer a road.

It is a front — but not all of it is burning.

I

Two continents

The Crownbridge connects Avalon's continent to the continent of Rath. It is not a portal hub, not a crossroads of worlds and not a normal fantasy bridge.

II

Country-sized span

Its surface holds roads, farms, settlements, supply depots, forts, watchtowers, ruins and long empty stretches where the ocean still feels impossibly far below.

III

Localized war

The fighting is concentrated toward the Rath-side reaches. The center of the bridge, Avalon and Rath itself are not fully consumed by devastation.

Primary theater

Huierdon Reach.

Huierdon Reach is not a single town or fort. It is a massive populated region on the Crownbridge, with roads, civilian settlements, defensive positions and military routes spread across an enormous artificial landstrip.

It is currently one of the most important theaters of the Avalon–Rath War.

Opening wound

The Fall of Ardent Gate.

Far from Avalon's quiet roads, war burns across the Crownbridge.

Rath demanded that Avalon abandon defensive agreements with Crownbridge settlements that had voluntarily sought Avalon's protection. Avalon refused to withdraw from those commitments.

Rath then attacked Ardent Gate, a fortified settlement within the protected Crownbridge territories. Avalonian forces reinforced the settlement and prioritized evacuating civilians while defenders held critical positions long enough for many to escape.

After Rath's attack on Ardent Gate, Avalon formally declared war. The declaration was a response to Rath's aggression, not the beginning of it.
