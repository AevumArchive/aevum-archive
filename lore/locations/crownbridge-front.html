War burns there

Avalon feels the rumor before it feels the fire.

The great bridge

The Crownbridge is not a simple crossing. It is a colossal span large enough to feel like a land of its own, joining one continent to another through stone, towers, roads and fortified reaches.

Distant war

Battle has taken root upon the bridge. The fighting is far enough that villages such as Neris still breathe normally, but every warfront has a way of sending shadows down the road.

Road whispers

Caravans arrive late, blades grow expensive, and sealed letters cross Avalon's roads more often than they should. The Crownbridge is distant, but distance does not silence a war.
