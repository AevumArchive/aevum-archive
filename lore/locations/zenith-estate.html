The ancestral seat

The estate preserves the old elven nobility of House Zenith through its records, starlight rites and instruction in Star Magic. It remained the house’s principal holding as its wider influence declined. Astera now claims it as her own and directs the house from there.

Jango was discovered in one of the associated villages and trained at the estate. His connection to the place grew through that training and his friendship with Astera.

Restoration and settlements

Neglected parts of the estate have returned to use. Residents have better living conditions and improved medical and magical care. The settlements associated with the estate receive greater support and protection.

Recovered records

Restoration uncovered sealed ancestral records. Their full contents and the estate’s exact historical obligations remain unclear.

Some of the records appear to preserve older darkness-aligned teachings. Astera is studying them alongside the discoveries from the Second Observatory.
