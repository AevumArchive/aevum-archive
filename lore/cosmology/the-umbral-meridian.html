The Umbral Meridian.

A hidden structure beneath ordinary distance: currents, crossings, thresholds, junctions, scars and wounds where impossible paths meet.

01A deliberate wound.02The Archive does not name an architect.

The severed current

A deliberate wound.

A major portion of the Meridian was deliberately severed. A foreign trace remains there, and Faaram's Soul Mark reacted to it. Astera did not discover who caused the severance.

Astera's connection

Recognized by the place.

A major Meridian location is now fully connected to Astera Zenith through her stabilized Second Sight, fully integrated Hollow Star and resonance. It may become a sanctuary, retreat or place of recovery in the future; it is not yet her home.

Unknowns preserved

The Archive does not name an architect.

The Meridian's creator, original purpose and age remain unknown. Its severer and the foreign trace's origin remain unknown. The later incident in which Astera disappeared does not establish that the Meridian caused it.
