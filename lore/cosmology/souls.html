Souls, death and return.

Death in Aevum is not one simple state. Body loss, soul damage, reincarnation and reconstruction must be kept separate.

Cosmology HubIchiro & Jiro
♢

Core rule

Soul destruction is permanent.

Body destruction alone does not always end a character forever.

01People tied to soul rules.

Opened rules

  • Destruction of the body does not necessarily mean permanent death.
  • Reincarnation or recreation may be possible while the soul remains.
  • Destruction of the soul is permanent.
  • Some souls possess unusual properties.
  • Repeated death can leave lasting consequences.

Known examples

  • Jiro possesses a Reinforced Soul.
  • Soul manipulation can encounter resistance.
  • Perfect soul reconstruction or revival has existed.
  • Resurrection can carry extreme consequences.
