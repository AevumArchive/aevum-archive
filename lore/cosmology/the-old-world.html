The Old World

Before the mapped universes and before Earth, there was the vast original planet remembered as the Old World. What remains is not a history in full. It is an archaeological outline written across surviving reality.

Before the twelve

A planet older than the current shape of creation.

The Old World was not one ordinary planet among many. It was the vast original planet whose breaking reshaped cosmology itself. Earth did not yet exist beneath its skies; neither did the later worlds in the forms now known.

Its remains do not sit neatly in one location. They became separated realities, damaged principles, ancestral traces and objects whose behavior still remembers laws that no longer govern the whole.

Archive caution

No first cause is opened.

No war, god, weapon, ritual or betrayal is confirmed as the cause of the Great Fragmentation. Any such answer remains speculation.

The terminal event

The Great Fragmentation.

Destruction on a cosmological scale.

The Old World was broken beyond restoration in an event preserved as the Great Fragmentation. The Archive can identify the event by its consequences, but not by the hand or force behind it. Most fragments were annihilated. Some became foundations for entirely new existence. Others survived as damaged remnants with histories that began long before their current inhabitants.

The Great Fragmentation is not the Multiversal Convergence. The Fragmentation ended the Old World's original unity; later convergences concern already separate realities colliding or being drawn together. Confusing the two erases the oldest boundary in the cosmological record.

The vanished immortals

Absence without a confirmed grave.

Immortals were associated with the Old World. After the Fragmentation they vanished from the opened record. Their survival, destruction or destination is unknown.

Descendants remain. That fact preserves lineage, not proof that the original immortals still exist.

Witness boundary

Most remnants were annihilated.

Survival is exceptional. A surviving object, lineage or world-fragment should never be treated as evidence that destruction was incomplete everywhere.

THE STONE REMEMBERS

Old World remnant

The Monoliths.

Across surviving reality stand ancient black structures whose origin points back toward the Old World. Their creators are unknown. They do not behave like monuments made only to be seen; they preserve or communicate fragments of law, memory, warning, names, commands and history.

A Monolith may be silent for generations and still not be inert. Contact is not a conversation in an ordinary language. Meaning arrives through the body, the environment and the mind around it.

Reported presences include pressure without a source, sudden silence, altered shadows, intrusive thoughts, recurring dreams, changes in nearby symbols, hesitation in magic, disturbed perception and animals behaving as if a shape exists where none can be seen. Not every Monolith produces every effect.

Some records suggest that certain Monoliths may be awakening, especially where reality has become unstable. The correlation is preserved; causation is not yet opened.
