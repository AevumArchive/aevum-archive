01

Tea in Tal

Ichiro's last clear memory comes from the old servant cellar in Tal: the large room he shared with Jiro, tea, and an ordinary conversation between brothers.

02

The warning that never arrived

They noticed someone approaching, possibly several people. Jiro immediately rushed out to warn Faaram. Ichiro tried to follow, became overwhelmingly tired and lost consciousness. Jiro never reached Faaram and was probably intercepted along the way.

03

Ichiro's black void

Ichiro remembers practically nothing of the long period that followed: only an immense, empty black space. He may have been conscious inside his own body, unable to control it. That interpretation remains a suspicion. The World Item's manipulation of his soul and body is confirmed.

04

The Orb in Jiro's chest

A mysterious Orb was implanted without Jiro's conscious knowledge. He does not remember how it happened. It influenced his will for a long time and could grant him additional power—enough for him to wound Bomir in the later fight. Its identity and the person who implanted it remain unknown.

05

Kazz chose to help

Kazz lent Faaram part of his power to help free the brothers. He wanted to break the control without unnecessary bloodshed. He had no intention of killing Ichiro or Jiro.

06

The cost of severance

Faaram broke the connection between Ichiro's soul and the World Item. The violent separation caused severe damage. Ichiro is himself again, but remains unconscious and recovering.

07

Bomir removed the Orb

During the final conflict, Bomir forcibly tore the Orb from Jiro's chest and ended its foreign control. Jiro lost his left arm while fighting Bomir and suffered a massive hole in his chest. He is critically injured. Whether he will survive is unknown.

The final confrontation

The space Poison bought.

A report before the fight

Before Faaram faced Ichiro, Poison told him she had found UWU. UWU appeared to be on the verge of death. The still-controlled Ichiro had reportedly discarded her after his reawakening because he saw no further use for her.

Poison then held Jiro back, keeping Faaram from being attacked by both brothers at once.

Bomir drew Jiro away

Jiro nearly reached Faaram during the final confrontation. Bomir used an unnamed ability to pull Jiro toward himself and away from Faaram. Faaram was able to speak with Ichiro after the World Item control broke, before Ichiro lost consciousness.

Thirty seconds of impacts

Bomir used Divinity Beyond Death against Jiro after feeling that Jiro could genuinely kill him. Its exact effect is unknown. For the next 30 seconds, Jiro received uninterrupted blows—estimated at a little over 200. Each impact was audible across roughly half the planet, with vibrations felt hundreds of kilometres away.

The barrage lasted thirty seconds. The ability's duration and nature remain unknown.

Poison's missing presence

After everything ended, Faaram could no longer sense Poison. She may have died trying to stop Jiro, but this is not confirmed. UWU's later survival and recovery are also unconfirmed.

Older history / Before the disappearance

Brothers before the lost years.

The older record describes their destroyed home planet and original universe, Faaram's restoration of the brothers through perfect souls, and Jiro's place as his right hand. Jiro survived the Aizen event with severe injuries.

Accounts of deliberate betrayal, Ichiro's supposed hidden hatred and Jiro's apparent full remembrance were written without knowledge of the prolonged control. They do not establish what either brother freely chose.

The old Death Note account and Keanu's Shadow Realm seal remain historical records. The events connecting those accounts to Ichiro's current survival have not been fully recorded. They do not override his confirmed present condition.
