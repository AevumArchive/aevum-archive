Ichiro and Jiro.

A brother story too important to hide only inside NPC cards: destroyed home, perfect souls, memory loss, hidden hatred and betrayal.

Known bond

Brothers.

Jiro followed Faaram; Ichiro remembered enough to hate him.

01

Before Faaram

Ichiro and Jiro destroyed their original home universe or planet together before powerful beings later killed them.

02

Perfect souls

Faaram eventually restored or recreated them using perfect souls. Jiro lost memory of their pre-death existence; Ichiro remembered everything.

03

The hidden conflict

Ichiro believed Faaram had taken his brother from him. He understood killing Faaram openly would cause Jiro to retaliate, so he pretended to serve faithfully while planning against him.

04

Limits of manipulation

Jiro's Reinforced Soul meant Ichiro could not simply rewrite him. Servant disappearances and the Timeo attack became part of the wider mystery.
