Known shape

Avalon currently serves as the main kingdom for the active characters. Its roads, towns, guild boards and trade routes are becoming the first stable structure of the Age of First Roads.

Current tension

The heartland still feels sheltered, but the Crownbridge war proves the age is not truly peaceful. Guild work, old ruins, strange contracts and distant battlefronts have begun pulling hidden threads into the open.

Capital sanctuary

Chapel of the First Light.

A sanctuary and chapel in Avalon's capital. Arnold helped construct it. Its religion, patron deity, clergy, founding date and deeper purpose remain unopened.

Wartime support

Allies beyond the army.

The independent Dusk Warden and Iron Vanguard adventurer guilds have sided with Avalon. They are supporting the kingdom in the war, but neither guild is part of Avalon's regular army.

Regional chart

Avalon, Crownbridge and Rath.

The opened road runs from Avalon across the Crownbridge toward Rath. Neris, Huierdon Reach and Ardent Gate remain tied to the established records below.
