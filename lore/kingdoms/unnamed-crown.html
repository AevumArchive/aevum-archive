Rath

The name is known. Banner, ruler and deeper motives remain protected by sealed records.

The Velvet Conquest claim

Rath accused Avalon of gaining influence across Crownbridge territories through diplomacy, trade and protection agreements rather than direct annexation. Avalon rejected that claim and treated the agreements as voluntary defense pacts.

Why the war began

Rath demanded that Avalon withdraw military protection from bridge settlements that had asked for aid. Avalon refused. Rath attacked Ardent Gate, and Avalon declared war in response.
