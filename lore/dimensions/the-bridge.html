I / THE HUB

More than one gate.

The Bridge functions as an enormous portal hub. Major portals stand alongside scattered access points, connecting distant worlds and spaces without making every destination public knowledge.

Not the Crownbridge.

Beyond the hub

The outer reaches are not safe passage.

The regions beyond the main portal hub are hellish and dangerous. Weak souls that remain there too long may be warped into demons. The Archive records the risk, not a guaranteed fate or a precise threshold.
