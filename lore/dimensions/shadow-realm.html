The Shadow Realm.

A highly dangerous otherworld strongly associated with Keanu, abnormal persistence and the earlier seal placed on Ichiro’s remains.

Classification

Abnormal dimension.

Its full physics remain unrecorded.

01Record boundary

Known access

  • Keanu could enter the Shadow Realm by releasing his connection to physical existence.
  • No general public route is known.
  • Ichiro's remains were brought there after his death.

Known dangers

  • The realm interacts abnormally with physical existence.
  • Approaching Ichiro's cursed remains is expected to destroy the body almost immediately.
  • Safe access, distance and duration remain unknown.
Witnessed

Keanu

Damaged shadow and Curse matter could gather again inside the realm. Destroying his visible body did not always mean ending him.

Sealed

Ichiro Seal

Keanu placed a powerful Curse directly upon Ichiro's remains and paid for it with the loss of his conscious existence.

Unknown

Current status

Ichiro is now awake, free from World Item control and at 3300 PL. The earlier Shadow Realm seal is a historical record; the events connecting it to his present survival remain unrecorded. The realm's safe access and the seal's full state are not established.

Record boundary

The Archive records observed access, persistence and danger. It does not claim complete dimensional laws, a public entrance or a safe recovery path.
