House Zenith.

An old elven noble line beyond Avalon whose silver rites and Star Magic survived while part of its deeper celestial history disappeared from the commonly preserved record.

Zenith Estate

Known history

House Zenith once carried greater influence among elven nobility. Over generations its reach diminished until only smaller lands, a few villages and the old family estate remained. Astera's recent discoveries now prove that this preserved history is incomplete.

Star-blood

The family preserves a rare inheritance known as Star Magic. It is a bloodline art studied through old tradition, but the Silent Gate showed that bloodline and raw power alone do not grant access to every part of that inheritance.

The Split Star

The opened sign of House Zenith is a divided star carried in silver and violet. It speaks of old celestial blood, measured grace and a lineage that remembers the night sky as inheritance.

House customs

Access beyond blood

The Silent Gate

The gate did not accept Astera merely because she carried House Zenith blood. Raw power was also insufficient. Her resonance, current state and connection to her staff were relevant, showing that the deeper inheritance is more complex than hereditary Star Magic alone.

Key / vessel significance

Astera's staff

The staff reacted to hidden Zenith material, the Veilgate, the Observatory and Astera's Dark-Astral resonance. The Hollow Star recognized a staff-linked fragment as incomplete or unfinished. Its original creator and complete purpose remain unrecovered.

Discovery, not conclusion

A Divided Celestial Heritage

The Hollow Star and Dark-Astral resonance imply that the family's older celestial legacy was not limited to the luminous understanding of Star Magic preserved today. This does not establish that House Zenith created the Hollow Star, worshipped the Void or fully understood the traditions now resurfacing.

Recovered ancient account

Stars beside darkness

An ancient unidentified presence encountered by Astera conveyed an account in which stars and darkness were not always treated as absolute opposites. It described later fear and light-centered teachings burying darkness-aligned sanctuaries, names and knowledge.

This remains recovered testimony from an unidentified source—not an omniscient history of House Zenith.

Astera Zenith

Astera is the young heir who left the safety of her home to understand her magic through the world itself, not only through books and instruction.

Jango

Jango was discovered in one of the villages tied to House Zenith lands. His talent for the blade brought him into the estate, where his path grew beside Astera's.
