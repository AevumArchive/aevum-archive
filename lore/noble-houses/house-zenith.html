Noble house record

House Zenith.

House Zenith is an old elven noble line from lands beyond Avalon. Its name is no longer widely spoken, but its halls still preserve traditions, silver rites and the rare bloodline of star magic.

Known seat

Zenith Estate

A quiet ancestral holding surrounded by a few old villages and fading influence.

Known history

House Zenith once carried greater influence among elven nobility. Over generations its reach diminished until only smaller lands, a few villages and the old family estate remained.

Star-blood

The family preserves a rare inheritance known as Star Magic. It is not a common school of magic, but a bloodline art passed through the house and studied through old tradition.

The Split Star

The opened sign of House Zenith is a divided star carried in silver and violet. It speaks of old celestial blood, measured grace and a lineage that remembers the night sky as inheritance.

House customs

Members of the house favor silver ornaments and old rites tied to starlight. Their halls preserve records, training and expectations that Astera now carries beyond the estate.

Astera Zenith

Astera is the young heir who left the safety of her home to understand her magic through the world itself, not only through books and instruction.

Jango

Jango was discovered in one of the villages tied to House Zenith lands. His talent for the blade brought him into the estate, where his path grew beside Astera's.
