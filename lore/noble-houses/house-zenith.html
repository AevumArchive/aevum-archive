House Zenith

An ancient elven house beyond Avalon, House Zenith preserved its silver rites and Star Magic through generations of declining influence. Astera Zenith, the Violet Eclipse, now leads its restoration from the ancestral estate. Her reforms have improved life in the holdings and divided the house over what it should teach.

Known history

House Zenith once carried greater influence among elven nobility. Over generations its reach diminished until only smaller lands, a few villages and the old family estate remained. The Second Observatory confirms that the preserved history was incomplete.

Star-blood

The family preserves a rare inheritance known as Star Magic. It is a bloodline art studied through old tradition, but the Silent Gate showed that bloodline and raw power alone do not grant access to every part of that inheritance.

The Split Star

The opened sign of House Zenith is a divided star carried in silver and violet. It speaks of old celestial blood, measured grace and a lineage that remembers the night sky as inheritance.

House customs

Members of the house favor silver ornaments and old rites tied to starlight. The estate still preserves records and training alongside its starlight rites.

Current leadership

Astera Zenith returned to the estate and claimed authority over House Zenith. She directs the restoration and the work of recovering, teaching and preserving the house’s magic. How authority passed to her remains undocumented.

The dispute concerns members of the house. Its wider political influence remains unconfirmed, and no external house rival has been recorded.

The Zenith Estate

The Zenith Estate is Astera’s ancestral home and the seat from which she governs. The house’s surviving holdings are small, with a few associated villages beyond Avalon. The restoration has reached the estate and its settlements.

The Zenith Restoration

Repairs brought neglected parts of the estate back into use. Residents received better living conditions and improved medical and magical care. The house increased its support and protection for the surrounding settlements.

Astera opened selected archives once reserved for senior members. Talented students can now receive magical instruction regardless of noble ancestry, and neglected kinds of Star Magic are again being studied.

The Zenith Dispute

Some members held to the house’s light-oriented interpretation of Star Magic. They questioned Astera’s embrace of the Hollow Star and the direction of her reforms, giving rise to a lasting internal dispute.

Unverified accounts Astera reportedly removed opponents from office and banished others. Some of the exiles are said to have returned and been killed by Astera. Their names reportedly disappeared from the record. The identities, circumstances and reliability of these accounts remain unverified.

Newly recovered house lore

The Second Observatory.

Astera opened a Zenith-linked complex whose existence no longer appeared in the house's normal known history. Modern members are not assumed to have known it survived.

Access beyond blood

The Silent Gate

The gate did not accept Astera merely because she carried House Zenith blood. Raw power was also insufficient. Her resonance, current state and connection to her staff were relevant, showing that the deeper inheritance is more complex than hereditary Star Magic alone.

Key / vessel significance

Astera's staff

The staff reacted to hidden Zenith material, the Veilgate, the Observatory and Astera's Dark-Astral resonance. The Hollow Star recognized a staff-linked fragment as incomplete or unfinished. Its original creator and complete purpose remain unrecovered.

Discovery, not conclusion

A Divided Celestial Heritage

The Hollow Star and Dark-Astral resonance imply that the family's older celestial legacy was not limited to the luminous understanding of Star Magic preserved today. This does not establish that House Zenith created the Hollow Star, worshipped the Void or fully understood the traditions now resurfacing.

Recovered ancient account

Stars beside darkness

An ancient unidentified presence encountered by Astera conveyed an account in which stars and darkness were not always treated as absolute opposites. It described later fear and light-centered teachings burying darkness-aligned sanctuaries, names and knowledge.

This remains recovered testimony from an unidentified source—not an omniscient history of House Zenith.

The Sealed Archives

Restoration uncovered sealed records from the house’s past. Some appear to preserve traces of an older understanding of darkness-aligned Star Magic. Astera is studying them alongside what she found in the Second Observatory.

Who removed these teachings from the commonly preserved history, why they were removed and what the recovered folios contain remain open questions.

Known individuals

Astera Zenith

Once the heir who left the estate to learn beyond its halls, Astera returned as the Violet Eclipse. She now leads House Zenith.

Jango

Jango was discovered in one of the villages tied to House Zenith lands. His talent for the blade brought him into the estate, where his path grew beside Astera's. His connection to the estate is through training and friendship.
