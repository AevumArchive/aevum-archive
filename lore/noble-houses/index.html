House record

Six parts of an opened name.

Every house is read through the same Aevum register without forcing different bloodlines into identical stories.

01 · MARK

Recognized sign

The symbol, colors and material language by which a house is identified.

02 · SEAT

Place of memory

The estate, hall, moving court or sanctuary where the house keeps its record.

03 · REACH

Living influence

Roads, villages, guild ties and people still touched by the house's decisions.

04 · CHARGE

Inherited duty

A responsibility the name is expected to preserve, whether welcomed or not.

05 · BONDS

Promises in force

Alliances, wards, friendships, debts and rival claims currently witnessed.

06 · BURDEN

Cost of continuity

The magic, debt, expectation or sealed history carried by the bloodline.
