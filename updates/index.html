They never chose to leave

Ichiro and Jiro were under outside control for a long time. Kazz lent Faaram power to help free them, without intending to kill either brother. Ichiro's World Item connection was severed; he was unconscious and recovering in the immediate aftermath. He has since awakened and reached 3300 PL through training. Bomir removed the Orb from Jiro's chest. In the immediate aftermath his left arm was lost, his injuries critical and his survival uncertain. He has since healed and awakened through training with Ichiro, reaching 3000 PL; the left arm remains permanently injured and the left hand needs special protection.

Poison reported finding UWU apparently near death, then held Jiro back to spare Faaram a simultaneous attack. Bomir drew Jiro away from Faaram and later used Divinity Beyond Death against him; its exact effect remains unknown. Faaram spoke with Ichiro after the control broke, before Ichiro lost consciousness. Faaram can no longer sense Poison—her possible death is unconfirmed.
