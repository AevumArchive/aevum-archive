01 · Twelvefold existence

One being. Twelve universes watched.

At some point in the past, Magnus Bane was divided into twelve separate parts. Each fragment is individually powerful enough to stand watch over—and protect—an entire universe.

The fragments are not recorded as political rulers. Their known responsibility is protection on a universal scale.

12

02 · Power scale

Far beyond an ordinary god.

Even one fragment must be understood as a major cosmic being. The complete Magnus Bane, if the twelve pieces were ever reunited, would therefore represent an existence vastly beyond ordinary divine entities.
