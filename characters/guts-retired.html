Kyron’s legacy

Guts became close friends with Kyron O’Gallhager, a master dragon hunter. Kyron taught him how to fight, how to hunt dragons, and where they were vulnerable.

Before Kyron vanished, he left Guts his house and both of his greatswords.

The king’s bodyguard

Guts joined the royal army and rose through its ranks until he stood among the king’s bodyguards. His reputation made him feared in rival kingdoms and among those who threatened the realm.

Unconfirmed account

A dragon brought down

One story claims Guts once hurled a greatsword at a dragon still high in the sky. The blade is said to have pierced its throat and brought it crashing to the ground.
