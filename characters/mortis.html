Notes

  • Mortis carried a vow born from a village destroyed by war and from the guilt of surviving it.
  • He did not seek the blade for glory; he sought strength enough to stand between the innocent and the next tyrant.
  • The Black Warden shaped his discipline, but his anger remained a danger he had to master.
  • His road ended in death, leaving behind a vow remembered darker than iron.
