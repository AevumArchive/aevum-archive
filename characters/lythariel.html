Lythariel

A wood elf alchemist who left family expectation, temple tradition and an arranged future behind to shape her own work through botany, blessing craft and experimental brewing.

Notes

  • Lythariel comes from a respected family of nature clerics, but chose alchemy over the life arranged for her.
  • Her brother understands that choice better than most, even if the pull of home has not fully faded.
  • She carries her life-work journal at her hip, filling it with herbs, blessings, observations and experiments.
  • Her craft is alchemy first, guided by botanical knowledge and limited nature-blessing magic.
  • Repeated deaths have left a permanent physical weakness whose exact weight is not openly shown.
  • After meeting Ciri, Lythariel became aware that witcher alchemy exists, but its secrets remain dangerous and unlearned.

Life-work Journal

Her most important possession is the book she carries at her hip: a growing record of plants, mixtures, blessings and unfinished formulae.

Unbound Path

She does not seek to copy her family tradition. Her purpose is to turn inherited blessing and gathered knowledge into a path of her own.

Scarred Vitality

Too many returns from death have weakened her body. The damage is real, but its full measure remains hidden.
