Wartime code

For life, not Avalon.

Lythariel is neither politically loyal to Avalon nor opposed to it. She will heal the wounded and prepare medicine, healing potions and protective supplies that cannot reasonably be used to harm another person.

She refuses offensive war potions, battlefield poisons, alchemical weapons and anything intended to injure or kill. This agreement does not make her a member of Avalon's war effort.

Agreement with Raoul

Materials for humanitarian craft.

Raoul accepted her conditions and will attempt to acquire the materials on her list. They are to be delivered directly to her home, without Lythariel paying the wartime procurement cost. A normal business arrangement may follow after the war.

The expense is to be drawn from an unidentified Grand Duke's resources for procurement—not given to Lythariel as a reward.

Practical Alchemy

She can currently prepare roughly five potion types and can restrict her work to healing potions. Some phials are sealed with wax so their contents cannot be detected by smell.

Material Shortage

War has made alchemical ingredients difficult to obtain. Her book collection is also diminished, though some volumes were lost or left behind simply because she could not carry everything. Books remain one of the finest rewards she can be offered.

Dangerous Contracts

She declined the combat work herself, but suggested a capable witch, another skilled adventurer whose name she could not recall, and a tavern owner accustomed to warriors and adventurers.

Home beyond the settlement

A secluded house among rare plants.

Lythariel built her large home far outside the nearby settlement, surrounded by nature and a notably good view. The interior is still being furnished. Unusual plants grow across the property, including specimens dangerous enough that visitors are warned not to touch them.

Known companions and beings around the property include Theodor and Tehla.

“I am interested in everything unknown.”

Unresolved casualty

Mira.

Lythariel previously aided an Avalon knight named Mira, whose eye was bloodied and heavily injured and whose leg was broken. Lythariel does not know whether Mira reached safety. Raoul could not identify her from the description.

Archive notes

  • Lythariel comes from a respected family of nature clerics, but chose alchemy over the life arranged for her.
  • Her brother understands that choice better than most, even if the pull of home has not fully faded.
  • She carries her life-work journal at her hip, filling it with herbs, blessings, observations and experiments.
  • Repeated deaths have left a permanent physical weakness whose exact weight is not openly shown.
  • After meeting Ciri, Lythariel became aware that witcher alchemy exists, but its secrets remain dangerous and unlearned.
