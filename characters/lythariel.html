Lythariel

A wood elf alchemist who left the expectations of her cleric family to pursue a life of botany, blessing craft and experimental brewing.

Race
Wood Elf
Path
Alchemist / Nature Cleric
Known Craft
Botany, brewing and limited blessing magic

ACTIVE

Notes

  • Lythariel comes from a respected cleric family devoted to a nature god, but her own calling bends toward alchemy.
  • She carries her life's work in a book at her hip, filling it with herbs, blessings and experiments.
  • Her strength is not in conquest, but in observation, careful hands and the patient search for new mixtures.
  • Her recipes should remain mysteries until she earns, studies or discovers them.
