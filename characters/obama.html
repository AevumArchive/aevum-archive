Notes

  • OBAMA began as a human, became Death's student and eventually rose into a Chaos Higherbeing.
  • His old height belonged to chaos, destruction and permanent erasure, but his loyalty to Peter became stronger than obedience to Death.
  • He once sacrificed himself for Peter, died, lost his soul and returned centuries later through a vessel shaped by a mysterious void figure.
  • That return stripped away much of his former existence: his higherbeing state, old chaos power and strengthened soul were taken from him.
  • The current OBAMA no longer seeks chaos for its own sake. His identity now turns around Authority, Stability, Protection, Rebuilding and Purpose.
  • Mandate is his core power. Mandate Flight works only inside his Mandate Land, and the returned Peter already bears one of his Mandates.
  • Ascend is gone. It is not locked, sealed or waiting to return.

Mandate

Mandate Land is claimed territory where OBAMA's authority becomes stronger, steadier and more difficult to contest.

New Order

His path is not a return to old chaos. It is authority shaped by consequence, protection and purpose.
