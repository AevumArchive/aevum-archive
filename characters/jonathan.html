Archive notes

  • Jonathan is a career military figure shaped by repeated combat rather than ceremony. His scars are part of the record, not decoration.
  • His eyes are technologically augmented and recognizable by their bright turquoise glow.
  • The gold Jojo insignia is worn on his right arm. It is a personal identifying mark rather than a rank badge.
  • His field presence is quiet and exhausted rather than theatrical: a professional who has seen enough violence to stop romanticizing it.
  • The Archive does not assign operations, affiliations or victories that have not been witnessed.
