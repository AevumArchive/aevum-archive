01 / The record

What the field left behind.

Archive notes

  • Jonathan is a career military figure shaped by repeated combat rather than ceremony. His scars are part of the record, not decoration.
  • What he witnessed and endured left him with severe PTSD and depression. The weight remains present even when the mission demands silence.
  • That suffering never made him less capable. Jonathan remained exceptionally skilled, controlled and dangerous in the work he knew best.
  • His eyes are technologically augmented and recognizable by their bright turquoise glow.
  • The gold Jojo insignia is worn on his right arm. It is a personal identifying mark rather than a rank badge.
  • His field presence is quiet and exhausted rather than theatrical: a professional who has seen enough violence to stop romanticizing it.
  • The Archive does not assign operations, affiliations or victories that have not been witnessed.

02 / The record

Discipline. Judgment. Survival.

Field discipline

Equipment, posture and habit all point toward a life organized around surviving the next operation.

What remained

The missions ended; their weight did not. His PTSD and depression are wounds carried beyond active service, not a measure of his ability.

Augmented sight

The turquoise ocular glow is the clearest visible sign that not everything about him is baseline human anymore.

Jojo mark

The right-arm insignia survives as a personal element within an otherwise practical military silhouette.
