01 / The record

Built on trust.

Notes

  • Jawohl founded Jawohl's Construction with help from Thomas Meyer, who paid the founding costs without taking ownership.
  • His first plot cost 8,000 dollars, and his next ambitions pointed toward larger land, more contracts and stronger infrastructure.
  • His path grew through materials, reputation, business growth and the trust needed to build for others.
  • He had only one life remaining near the end of his known road.
  • Jawohl completed the Silent Fault / World Anchor event and was remembered around the 82nd completion.
