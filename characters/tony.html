Notes

  • Tony fought in the Anchorage War on the American side before becoming a gifted engineer tied to Titanfall-era technology.
  • His soul and awareness passed through multiple bodies across his long existence.
  • He became Overseer, or at least a central authority, of Vault 8: a vast vault with civilian quarters, high-value residences and a massive military sector.
  • J.A.R.V.I.S. was more than a tool to Tony; he treated the intelligence like a buddy.
  • Older estimates placed Tony himself around PL 350-500, while Vault 8 as a military power stood far higher as a threat.
