Origin timeline / The life he lived

The Making of an Everthorn.

Faaram became one of the strongest elves alive. His first lessons came on a battlefield.

  1. I / First war

    What the field taught him

    Faaram was still young by elven standards when he first marched to war. He knew how to use a weapon. Then he watched warriors with years more experience die within seconds. An elegant strike was worth little if it left him open. Hesitate once, and there might be no second exchange.

    He came away from each fight with something he could use. A stance. A rhythm. A mistake. Techniques that had wounded him once grew easier to spot. Repeated attacks became patterns, and patterns gave him openings. By the time he returned home, he had already begun to fight the way an Everthorn would.

  2. II / Elven Circle

    Centuries under arms

    His actions in the war earned him a place in the Elven Circle. He trained there for decades, then centuries. He studied weapons until their movements became instinct and fought masters whose experience dwarfed his own. In time, a shift in an opponent's stance could tell him what they were about to do. Even after he surpassed his teachers, he kept looking for someone who could show him where he was still vulnerable.

    Sunrise practice was another matter. Faaram might arrive after everyone had started, forget a meeting outright, or spend an afternoon putting off a task. He could recall the way an opponent shifted their weight decades ago and still have no idea where he'd left a document ten minutes earlier. Give him something that held his attention and he'd train for days.

  3. III / The Demi-God

    A name beyond the Circle

    His name spread well beyond the Circle. Then Faaram faced a Demi-God and killed it. The fight demanded everything he had spent centuries learning. He left it stronger than he entered it, with more to learn.

  4. IV / The invasion of S'klrdor

    Five stood. Four remained.

    When the Demon Lord S'klrdor invaded the elven lands, five legendary warriors rose to stop him. Faaram was one of them. Together they became the Everthorns. One fell in the war. The other four brought down S'klrdor.

    Faaram went back to training. A defeat showed him where he could be hurt. A victory gave him a chance to study the openings he'd missed.

  5. V / The four leaders

    The empty seat at council

    In time, Faaram became one of four great leaders entrusted with guiding the elves across the world. Younger elves told stories of council meetings that began with his chair empty. Giving him more meetings had mostly given him more meetings to forget.

    When his people were in danger, they knew who would stand with them. The elf who could forget a council meeting was the same one who had fought for them through two wars. When it mattered, he was there. Usually late. But there.

What he carried from his first war

Observe. Endure. Adapt. Overcome.

Every second you fail to kill an Everthorn gives them another lesson they can use against you.
