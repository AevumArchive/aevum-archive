01 / The record

The hearth he inherited.

The Carter Hearth

Elias' tavern, William's work.

Elias—William's uncle and the tavern's original owner—left the building to William after his death. William has heavily rebuilt the old cellar while preserving enough of it for Elias' close friend Raoul to recognize the original structure.

Raoul, who knew about William through Elias but met him only after Elias died, called the renovated cellar a real masterpiece.

Civilian war economy

Short supplies, continued craft.

Wartime shortages have made the materials and equipment needed for distillation harder to obtain. William still intends to expand what he can produce and offer. After his donation to the front, Raoul believed some of the needed resources could probably be arranged.

02 / The record

Beyond the tavern door.

Voluntary support

Supplies for Crownbridge.

William offered physical supplies from his own stock to Avalon's fighters, believing they were needed more at the front. Raoul will arrange collection through an intermediate depot, a process expected to take roughly one or two days before onward transport.

This material donation is separate from Lythariel's strictly humanitarian agreement.

A lasting burden.

Permanent condition

Weakening.

Repeated deaths and damage left William with a permanent WEAKENING. Worn armor grants him significantly less Armor Points and Armor Toughness than it otherwise would. This is not a minor temporary penalty.

Archive notes

  • William learned the tavern trade from his uncle, who left him both a building and a responsibility.
  • He is strongest behind a counter, where a steady voice and a clean mug can change the mood of a room.
  • His first talents are brewing, listening and keeping a hearth alive rather than battle or grand magic.
  • His tavern license, taxes and local reputation should matter because his story grows through work, guests and rumor.
