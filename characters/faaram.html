After the final conflict

They never chose to leave him.

Ichiro and Jiro spent a long time under outside control. Much of what they did was not their own choice. Faaram finally knows that they were victims, not willing betrayers.

Kazz lent Faaram part of his power to help free the brothers. He sought to break the control without unnecessary bloodshed, not to kill either of them.

IchiroFree · UnconsciousRecovering from the severed World Item connection.JiroFree · CriticalOrb removed. Left arm lost. Survival uncertain.

Before his final fight with Ichiro, Poison told Faaram she had found UWU apparently near death, discarded by the still-controlled Ichiro after his reawakening. Poison then held Jiro back so that Faaram would not have to face both brothers at once.

When Jiro nearly reached him, Bomir pulled Jiro toward himself with an unnamed ability. Faaram was able to speak with Ichiro after the control broke, before Ichiro lost consciousness.

Afterward, Faaram could no longer sense Poison. She may have died holding Jiro back, but her death is unconfirmed.

For now, Faaram waits for Ichiro to wake—and to learn whether Jiro ever will. Poison's silence leaves another fate unknown.
