Notes

  • Faaram is remembered as The Faceless, a name tied to ritual craft, servants and hidden authority.
  • His magic leaned toward command, resurrection themes and the shaping of forces that did not always belong to ordinary life.
  • The seal around his path does not erase him; it keeps his former weight from walking as the active vessel.
