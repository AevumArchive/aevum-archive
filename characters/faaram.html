Notes

  • Faaram is remembered as The Faceless, a name tied to ritual craft, servants and hidden authority.
  • His preserved strength is recorded around Power Level 900.
  • Faaram and OBAMA are remembered as close friends whose bond stretches across more than a thousand years.
  • Nocturne is bound to his former path as a World Item-class sword, carrying a presence far beyond ordinary weapons.
  • The Runic Piercer once belonged to Faaram, but its place in the world is now lost.
  • Overlord's Grace remains recorded as a rare mark on his story, not as an object carried in hand.
