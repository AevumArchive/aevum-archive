Notes

  • Faaram is remembered as The Faceless, a name tied to ritual craft, servants and hidden authority.
  • His current body is a Champion vessel whose name remains unknown; the preserved scar remains part of his appearance.
  • His current strength is recorded at Power Level 908.
  • Faaram and OBAMA are remembered as close friends whose bond stretches across more than a thousand years.
  • Nocturne is bound to his former path as a World Item-class sword, carrying a presence far beyond ordinary weapons.
  • The Azure World Item Shard is a crafting material, not a relic or separate World Item.
  • His resurrection history, former servants and newer servant order remain connected records.
  • Overlord's Grace is the rare Mythic mark left by Faaram's encounter with the Overlord. Faaram is its eighth recorded bearer worldwide; its deeper mechanics remain sealed.
  • The Archive records a changed likeness after the Multiversal Convergence. Whether the fusion reshaped his vessel remains uncertain.

Witnessed interaction

Astera's awakened soul

Faaram has been observing Astera Zenith. He placed a mark directly on her soul that previously limited her power and deeper perception. After she fully integrated the Hollow Star, he took a major but incomplete portion of her soul. The Hollow Star did not transfer cleanly. The Mark’s present state is unresolved.

A black-violet flash engulfed Astera and she disappeared. Faaram remembers the flash and disappearance, not their cause or her destination. He retains an incomplete soul portion; residual Hollow-Star or astral resonance may remain, but no specific benefit is confirmed.

Purpose remains sealed

Limits not yet crossed

Faaram had indicated that a soul in this condition may cross limits. The later extraction did not give him Astera’s entire soul or a clean transfer of the Hollow Star.

The flash, Astera’s fate and the exact residual effects remain unknown. Reclaiming His Place is complete; no next quest is assigned.
