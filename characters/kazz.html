01 / Spirit Realm

Spirit Domain

A soul that will never fade.

The Faceless killed Kazz's body. His soul endured and remains in the Spirit Realm. It will only perish if it is killed in a duel.

Even after his bodily death, his power is estimated at 4,000 PL. He still bears a grudge against the Faceless.

02 / The fight against Ichiro

A debt to Tempest.

From the Spirit Realm

During the fight against Ichiro, Kazz shielded Faaram Tempest's soul from Ichiro's attacks. He then severed the World Item's link to Ichiro's soul. With the link broken, Ichiro came to his senses.

A debt of honor

In Kazz's eyes, Faaram Tempest was the one who killed the Faceless. Kazz felt he owed Tempest for that alone. When the fight came, he paid the debt.

03 / The samurai code

The duel Kiba dishonored.

Kazz lives by a strict samurai code. He holds justice and honor above all else and opposes needless killing.

Kiba drew a gun midway through a sword duel and shot Kazz's master dead. Kazz will never forgive him for it. Kiba is the only person Kazz means to kill.

04 / The Kurogane family

A blade from the island.

The Kurogane katana

The Kurogane family gifted Kazz a special katana and welcomed him to the island where the Tengoku clan's main village stands.

Kenka Kurogane

Kazz formed a close friendship with Kenka, a formidable fighter and the son of Zenith Kurogane, head of the Tengoku clan.
