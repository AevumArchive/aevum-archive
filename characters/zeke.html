Archive notes

  • Zeke earned entry into a special-operations unit through exceptional military training and capability.
  • His record is defined by professionalism rather than supernatural authority.
  • During a temporal-rift incident, Zeke disappeared from known continuity.
  • No body was recovered and no reliable evidence confirms his death.
  • The Archive therefore does not classify him as Fallen, Dead or Retired. His status is Lost / Unknown.
  • A temporal disappearance leaves an additional uncertainty ordinary missing-person records do not have: “where” may not be the only missing coordinate.

Lost / Unknown

The distinction is deliberate. Zeke is absent, not confirmed dead.

Temporal rift

The event removed him from ordinary continuity. What lies beyond that absence remains unknown.
