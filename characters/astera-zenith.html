Notes

  • House Zenith keeps an old elven bloodline whose name has faded from many courts, but not from its own halls.
  • Astera studies star magic as inheritance, burden and calling rather than simple spellcraft.
  • She left the safety of her home because books alone cannot teach the full weight of the stars.
  • Duality marks her as unusually rare: two possible futures may answer her choices, and both deserve their own portraits.
