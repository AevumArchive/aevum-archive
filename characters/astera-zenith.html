Current era

The Violet Eclipse

Head of House Zenith

Astera once left the Zenith Estate to study magic beyond the limits of her house. She returned to find that the knowledge she had recovered called into question much of what the house still taught. The estate is now the seat of her authority.

Under her leadership, repairs, better care and wider access to magical education have improved life across the holdings. Her decision to reopen parts of the celestial inheritance has also brought her into conflict with members of her own house.

Personality & Character

The young heir questioned the received accounts of Star Magic and treated unfamiliar powers with curiosity. When she encountered the ancient presence associated with the Dark Star, she wondered whether it might be alone. During the Trial of Self, she chose Jango as an anchor to a life she had made beyond the expectations of her bloodline.

Astera now directs her own investigations and is willing to dismantle traditions that keep knowledge out of reach. The Mark and Faaram’s extraction sharpened her resolve to decide what happens to her magic and her soul. Her judgment and ambitions remain her own.

Her care for the households and settlements she governs is visible in the reforms. She is more selective about trust and more severe toward those she sees as a threat to her rule. The accounts of what happened to the returned exiles remain unverified.

House Zenith / Current Era

The Zenith Council.

Astera now presides over the house whose traditions once shaped her own life. Her authority extends beyond magical knowledge to the decisions that determine its future.

Her leadership has brought substantial improvements to the estate and its inhabitants, although her increasingly uncompromising approach to opposition remains a subject of dispute.

Return to Zenith

  1. The Unrecorded Return

    After Faaram took a major but incomplete portion of her soul, a black-violet flash engulfed Astera and she disappeared from the known record. She rarely speaks of that interval. Where she went, how she survived and what she remembers remain undocumented.

    By the time she returned to the Zenith Estate, her command of Dark Astral Magic had deepened. The wound beneath her awakened left eye was still visible.

  2. The Claim of Zenith

    The Second Observatory had shown Astera how much of the house’s celestial inheritance lay beyond the teachings still in use. On her return she challenged the authority that restricted its study and took leadership of House Zenith. The estate she once left became her seat, and she now speaks of it as her own.

    The transfer of authority from the previous leadership remains undocumented.

  3. The Zenith Restoration

    Astera restored neglected parts of the estate and directed resources toward better living conditions, medical and magical care, and the support and protection of nearby settlements tied to the house.

    She opened selected archives once reserved for senior members, resumed study of neglected forms of Star Magic and made magical education available to talented students regardless of noble ancestry.

  4. The Zenith Dispute

    Some within House Zenith held to its light-oriented Star Magic and regarded the Hollow Star as a danger to the house. Astera’s reforms brought this disagreement into open internal conflict.

    Astera stripped several opponents of their positions and banished them from the lands under her authority.

    Unverified accounts Some exiles are said to have returned in defiance of the ban and been killed by Astera. Their names reportedly no longer appear in the house records. Their identities, the circumstances of any deaths and the reliability of these accounts remain unverified.

  5. The Sealed Archives

    Restoration brought sealed ancestral records to light. Some appear to come from a period when darkness-aligned Star Magic was understood differently. Alongside the Second Observatory and the Hollow Star, the records give Astera a way to investigate what was removed from the house’s history, and by whom.

    The full contents and meaning of the records are still unknown.

Dark Star contact

Recovered testimony

  • An ancient unidentified presence conveyed memories or knowledge reaching millennia into the past.
  • Its account described traditions in which stars and darkness were once understood alongside one another rather than as absolute enemies.
  • Later fear and light-centered doctrine buried darkness-aligned sanctuaries, teachings, names and knowledge.
  • This is recovered testimony, not omniscient history; the presence's identity and faction remain unknown.
  • Astera met the Dark Star with unusual empathy, wondering whether it was alone instead of assuming hostility.
  • The contact ended abruptly. Astera sensed Faaram watching around that moment, but it is not confirmed that he caused the interruption.
