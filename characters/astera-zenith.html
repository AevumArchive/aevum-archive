Dark Star contact

Recovered testimony

  • An ancient unidentified presence conveyed memories or knowledge reaching millennia into the past.
  • Its account described traditions in which stars and darkness were once understood alongside one another rather than as absolute enemies.
  • Later fear and light-centered doctrine buried darkness-aligned sanctuaries, teachings, names and knowledge.
  • This is recovered testimony, not omniscient history; the presence's identity and faction remain unknown.
  • Astera met the Dark Star with unusual empathy, wondering whether it was alone instead of assuming hostility.
  • The contact ended abruptly. Astera sensed Faaram watching around that moment, but it is not confirmed that he caused the interruption.

Inheritance and training

  • Recognized as a Mythic Instructor with Star Magic and sword training.
  • Her staff grows with her and reacted to the Veilstones, Veilgate, Observatory and Dark-Astral resonance.
  • The Hollow Star recognized a staff-linked Dark-Astral fragment as incomplete or unfinished; its complete historical function remains unknown.
  • Thalanil, the hidden village and cult interest remain tied to her wider road.
  • Magical Understanding and Cleansing Breath remain witnessed abilities.
