Notes

  • Raven first gained reputation through raider kills and sold dog tags.
  • Karstadt later hired him for road safety and security work.
  • His greatest confirmed raider success was clearing a huge outpost and killing two Raider Bosses.
  • A Raider Boss with an AA-12 caused one of his known deaths; his last reliable state is one life remaining.
  • Raven began building a hideout, with Viktor Gartno inspecting its weaknesses and preparing reinforced security work.
  • A Rogue PMC Remnant became one of his more serious modern threats.

Firearm Discipline

Overall Firearm Proficiency is remembered at Elite I — Level 4, with LMG Handling at Elite I — Level 1.

Hideout Work

His base required a reinforced main door, security corridor, deeper protection and better defensive separation.

Threat Profile

Raven remains dangerous because of training, recoil control, equipment, patience and clean execution.
