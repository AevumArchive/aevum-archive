01 · Character record

Loyalty without ambition.

Fritz had little independent personality or ambition beyond loyalty to OBAMA. Despite his immense strength, OBAMA rarely made meaningful use of him; much of his existence was spent simply remaining present.

02 · Witnessed abilities

Heat made physical.

Three abilities are recorded. The Archive does not extend their range, effect or purpose beyond what was witnessed.

I

Constant field

Heatwave Sphere

Fritz constantly radiated extreme heat in a sphere of roughly 50 meters around himself. Temperatures near 700°C caused surrounding material and living beings to burn simply by remaining close.

II

Physical enhancement

Hellknight Empowerment

Visible energy gathered around his hands—especially the knuckles—and feet, greatly increasing his speed, jumping ability and unarmed close-combat power. His fists became his primary weapons.

III

Targeted execution

Incineration Execution

Fritz charged a nearby target, drove an elbow into the chest, then placed a hand against the target and incinerated them at approximately 18,000–20,000°C. Ordinary targets were reduced to ash.

Archive distinction

The final battle is unwitnessed.

Fritz's cause of death remains unknown. Ichiro is suspected as a possible cause, but no record confirms his involvement.
