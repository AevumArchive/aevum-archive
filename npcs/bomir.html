Archive notes

  • Bomir belongs to the Götterfamilie, an alliance rather than a literal biological family.
  • He is regarded as godlike because the power behind the unassuming figure has justified that description.
  • Enski periodically challenges Bomir and refers to him as an “old man,” despite being an ancient Higherbeing himself.
  • That detail fuels speculation about Bomir's age, but the Archive does not turn speculation into ancestry.
  • Age, origin and the reason for his longevity remain unconfirmed.
