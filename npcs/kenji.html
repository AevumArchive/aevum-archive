A unit of his own

Kenji is a member of the Tengoku Clan outside the Kurogane family. He commands his own small unit within the clan and relies on his tantō alone in combat.

Spiritual Power

How the clan adopted the name

Kazz once called it Spiritual Power while speaking with Kenji. Kenji had only ever known it by another name. The new term struck him and the other Tengoku members as so fitting that they adopted it.
