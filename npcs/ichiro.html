01 / After the severance

Free.
Not yet awake.

Confirmed

The connection is broken.

The World Item manipulated Ichiro's soul and body. Kazz lent Faaram part of his power to help break its connection to Ichiro's soul. Kazz wanted to avoid unnecessary bloodshed; he did not intend to kill either brother.

Recovery

The damage remains.

The violent separation caused severe damage. Ichiro is himself again, but remains unconscious and must recover. Faaram is waiting for him to wake.

II

02 / Soul and captivity

A soul held
inside its own body.

No sky.
No walls.Only blackness.

After the cellar, Ichiro remembers almost nothing of the long years that followed. There is only an immense black void: an empty space with no recognizable surroundings.

He may have remained conscious inside his own body, unable to control it. This is a suspected explanation of that void, not a confirmed account of every moment.

Soul Manipulation remains his recorded power. But Ichiro himself was also under World Item control. The acts committed through him cannot simply be treated as his freely chosen intent.

III

03 / The last clear memory

Tea in the cellar.
Then nothing.

The brothers' shared history ↗

  1. Before the lost years

    The older history records that the brothers destroyed their home planet and original universe, and that Faaram later restored them through perfect souls. This precedes the disappearance now being understood.

  2. The room in Tal

    Ichiro's last clear memory comes from the old days in Tal. He and Jiro sat in the large room they shared in the servant cellar, drinking tea and talking normally.

  3. Someone approaching

    They noticed someone approaching—possibly more than one person. Jiro immediately rushed out to warn Faaram. Ichiro meant to follow.

  4. The sudden sleep

    An overwhelming exhaustion stopped him. Before he could follow Jiro, Ichiro lost consciousness.

  5. The lost years

    A World Item controlled his soul and body for a long time. He remembers practically nothing of that period beyond the vast black emptiness. What looked like willing betrayal was not freely chosen.

  6. Severance

    With power lent by Kazz, Faaram broke the connection between Ichiro's soul and the World Item. Ichiro is free, unconscious and recovering.
