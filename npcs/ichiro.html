II

02 / Defining power

Soul Manipulation

Loyalty.
Trust. Belief.The shape of identity itself.

Ichiro did not need to break a body first. His Soul Manipulation reached into the structures beneath a person's loyalties, convictions and sense of self.

He worked through Faaram's servants over time, turning trust against its source. Timeo was drawn into the betrayal long before its full shape was revealed.

Jiro's Reinforced Soul resisted Ichiro's direct control. His own brother could not be reached as easily as the others. The conspiracy became slower, more patient and more elaborate.

III

03 / The long betrayal

Loyalty was the disguise.

Open the brothers' shared history ↗

  1. Before the servants

    Ichiro and Jiro were brothers before Faaram ever touched their souls. Together they destroyed their home planet and eventually their original universe, before both were killed by powers strong enough to end them.

  2. Perfect souls

    Faaram later revived or re-created both through perfect souls. Jiro returned without the old memories. Ichiro remembered everything.

  3. The hidden hatred

    Ichiro believed Faaram had taken his brother from him. He pretended to serve, concealing his hatred while preparing Faaram's downfall from within.

  4. The servants turn

    His influence spread through the servants. He turned Timeo against Faaram and eventually revealed the shape of the betrayal.

  5. A failed reunion

    Ichiro restored Jiro's memories, hoping the truth would bring his brother back. Instead, the returning past overwhelmed Jiro. He stood apart from the final clash.

  6. The Death Note

    Ichiro was killed by the Death Note. What remained of him was still dangerous enough that Keanu carried the remains into the Shadow Realm and cursed them there.

04 / Shadow Realm

The Soul Seal

Keanu became the curse that kept the remains sealed.

He laid an enormous curse directly upon Ichiro's remains, paying for it with the slow loss of his own conscious existence.

The remains lie cursed within the Shadow Realm. Reaching them is considered nearly impossible. Approaching them is expected to destroy the body almost immediately.

V

05 / The surviving trace

One bone.
An unfinished plan.

UWU was Faaram's first servant. Believed lost, she had secretly been restored by Ichiro and still carries one of his finger bones.

That fragment anchors his influence beyond his death. UWU remains alive, altered and compromised; her true loyalty and the extent of her autonomy cannot yet be separated from his control.

Ichiro appears to have planned beyond his own death. The body is dead, but the plan has not fully stopped moving.
