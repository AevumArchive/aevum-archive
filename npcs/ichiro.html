Notes

  • Ichiro and Jiro were brothers before Faaram ever touched their souls.
  • The brothers destroyed their home planet and eventually their original universe before both were killed by powers strong enough to end them.
  • Faaram later revived or re-created both through perfect souls. Jiro returned without the old memories; Ichiro remembered everything.
  • Ichiro believed Faaram had taken his brother from him. His hatred did not burn loudly at first. It waited.
  • For roughly two real-world years of play, Ichiro pretended to serve while preparing Faaram's downfall from within.
  • His Soul Manipulation could alter loyalty, trust, belief and the deeper structure of identity itself.
  • Jiro's Reinforced Soul resisted Ichiro's direct control, forcing Ichiro into a slower and more elaborate conspiracy.
  • Ichiro manipulated servants over time, turned Timeo against Faaram and finally revealed the shape of the betrayal.
  • He restored Jiro's memories, hoping the truth would bring his brother back. Instead, Jiro was overwhelmed and stood apart from the final clash.
  • Ichiro was killed by the Death Note, but his remains were too dangerous to leave unsealed.
  • Keanu laid an enormous curse directly upon Ichiro's remains and paid for it with the slow loss of his own conscious existence.
  • The remains now lie cursed within the Shadow Realm. Reaching them is considered nearly impossible, and approaching them is expected to destroy the body almost immediately.
  • Ichiro appears to have planned beyond his own death. UWU, Faaram's first servant, was secretly restored by Ichiro and still carries one of Ichiro's finger bones.
  • That bone sustains Ichiro's influence. The body is dead, but the plan has not fully stopped moving.
