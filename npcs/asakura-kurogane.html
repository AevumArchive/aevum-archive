Asakura Kurogane

Kenka Kurogane’s older sister by six years. Asakura appears to wield formidable Spiritual Power.

Family

Spiritual healing

Asakura practices a secret form of Spiritual Power that allows her to heal the body.

Healing wounds
Asakura can use her Spiritual Power to heal an injury and restore the damaged tissue.
Regenerating body parts
In certain cases, her healing can restore a part of the body that was lost entirely. The circumstances that allow that level of recovery remain unknown.

Kazz’s kimono

Asakura gave Kazz the kimono he wears. He became a close friend of her younger brother Kenka after the family welcomed him to the island where the Tengoku clan’s main village stands.

The Kurogane family also gifted Kazz a special katana.
