Uwubama

A clone of Obama who grew far beyond the limits of an ordinary duplicate and repeatedly rewrote reality so Obama and his friends could continue.

Notes

  • Uwubama learned to travel through time and manipulate timelines, realities and the wider multiverse.
  • The full limit of his abilities is still not known.
  • When Temmie Town was destroyed, a later playthrough could begin with the city and its people restored.
  • Those returns were not simple resets. Uwubama had altered reality behind the scenes.
  • His repeated violations of time and reality eventually drew the intervention of the Time Gods.
  • They erased him almost completely, but a fragment of his brain still exists and leaves restoration theoretically possible.

Protector

His intervention centered on one purpose: Obama and the people around him should survive and continue.

Eternal City

Uwubama's alterations are the reason Temmie Town could return and develop across broken timelines.

Unknown reach

The archive knows what he changed, but not the complete range of what he could do.
