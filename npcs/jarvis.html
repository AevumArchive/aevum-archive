Notes

  • J.A.R.V.I.S. is not treated as a disposable system. Tony trusted him as a buddy.
  • He oversees Vault 8 and the technical systems of every known facility belonging to Tony.
  • His intelligence can upload itself into a vast cloud if a physical AI body or housing is destroyed.
  • From that cloud he can be downloaded into a new compatible body or system, preserving operational continuity.
  • His current architecture carries six of the strongest AI components available to him.
  • Among the server AIs currently known to the archive, his approximate standing is ninth.

Facility mind

Vault 8 is his central responsibility, but his reach follows Tony's infrastructure wherever it exists.

Cloud continuity

Destroying one housing does not end J.A.R.V.I.S.; the intelligence can retreat, persist and return.
