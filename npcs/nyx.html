Notes

  • Nyx observes, evaluates and speaks when silence would be easier.
  • She understood that Faaram could not reclaim his place while remaining entirely absent from the world.
  • Her counsel around Jiro forced Faaram to consider what taking back a lost pillar would truly mean.
  • Current records place her as the strongest of Faaram's three newer servants.
  • Nox may alter a battle. Rapax may retrieve what others cannot reach. Nyx may alter the decision that causes the battle at all.
  • Her restraint should not be mistaken for obedience. Nyx can oppose a method while still serving the larger purpose.
  • Among the newer servants, she is the clearest sign that Faaram's power is rebuilding through thought as much as force.

Voice

Calm, restrained and willing to challenge the path her master chooses.

Phase

Phase II, Resonance III. Still advancing.

Danger

Strategy, timing and the ability to move Faaram from secrecy toward presence.
