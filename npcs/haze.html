Sirius Galaxy

Two years away from Earth.

Haze was human once. One day he woke on a hostile planet in the Sirius Galaxy, the most dangerous galaxy in the universe. He had no idea how he got there.

He remembers his skin burning and melting off his body as he regained consciousness, and the agony that came with it. He remembers nothing else from the roughly two years he spent on the planet. Eventually he woke up back on Earth as though nothing had happened.

Radiation and abilities

What came back with him.

Haze seems to have become a mutant on that planet. He constantly gives off intense radiation of an unfamiliar kind. It does no harm to those nearby.

Since his return he has been able to move at extraordinary speeds and open small Gates. What else he can do is still unclear.
