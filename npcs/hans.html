01 · Witnessed nature

Violence without command.

Hans did not require an order from OBAMA to kill. The Archive records no hidden nobility, misunderstood motive or sympathetic explanation for his behavior.

Known appearance

A body entirely of flesh

Hans was wholly fleshy and monstrous. His defining feature was a single enormous toothed mouth that extended around toward the back of his head.

Known behavior

Predation by choice

He sometimes entered villages on his own, slaughtered the inhabitants and consumed them. He was known to target Villager children first and to carry several kilograms of Villager flesh away as food for later.

Archive distinction

The killer is unknown.

Hans' cause of death has not been confirmed. Ichiro is suspected as a possible cause during the old era, but the Archive records that only as suspicion—not fact.
