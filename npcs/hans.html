01 · Preserved record

A remnant made of flesh.

Hans belongs to an older and darker period of OBAMA's history. The record confirms his service and death, but does not pretend to know the powers or history it never witnessed.

Known appearance

No human face

His heavy body is formed from dark red, maroon and near-black flesh. A single enormous mouth wraps around the head, its rows of teeth replacing any conventional human features.
