01 · Archive summary

The servant who was never gone.

UWU was Faaram's first servant. Faaram and those around him believed that she had died. The opened record now shows that she survived or was restored long ago and later fell beneath Ichiro's influence.

The likenesses above preserve her earlier known states. Before the final confrontation, Poison reported finding UWU apparently near death, discarded after the still-controlled Ichiro no longer saw any use for her. The images do not establish her condition after that report.

02 · The First Servant

Before every later bond, there was UWU.

Her place beside Faaram is not a later appointment or a recovered title. UWU was his first servant—the earliest opened name in that lineage. What remains of that original loyalty after prolonged manipulation has not been safely determined. Ichiro was himself under foreign control; his liberation does not confirm that UWU is free.

03 · Ichiro's control

A will still unwitnessed.

The earlier record ties UWU's soul manipulation to Ichiro and a finger bone she carries. It is now confirmed that Ichiro himself was under World Item control for a long time. How much of the manipulation was imposed through him—and whether UWU's bond has changed since his liberation—remains unknown.

04 · Temporal affinity

A will moving against the hour.

Temporal manipulation has been repeatedly associated with UWU. The Archive records the affinity without claiming a complete catalogue of its limits or forms.
