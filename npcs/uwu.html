02 · The First Servant

Before every later bond, there was UWU.

Her place beside Faaram is not a later appointment or a recovered title. UWU was his first servant—the earliest opened name in that lineage. What remains of that original loyalty beneath Ichiro's control has not been safely determined.

03 · Ichiro's control

A command that survived its author.

Ichiro manipulated UWU's soul and placed one of his finger bones within her reach. The fragment anchors his influence beyond his death, allowing the control to persist while the true extent of UWU's remaining will stays unknown.

04 · Temporal affinity

A will moving against the hour.

Temporal manipulation has been repeatedly associated with UWU. The Archive records the affinity without claiming a complete catalogue of its limits or forms.
