01 · Witnessed abilities

A body that becomes the escape.

Kimiko's complete capabilities remain unknown. The techniques below are witnessed facts, not an estimate of her full Power Level or limits.

I

Evasion

Spider Swarm Evasion

When attacked, Kimiko can break her physical form apart into a swarm of small spiders, allowing the attack to pass through or miss what had been her body. She then reconstructs herself from the swarm.

This is not ordinary teleportation: her body physically disperses before reforming.

II

Silent traversal

Silent Surface Movement

Kimiko can adhere to walls and ceilings with her feet and move across them freely without producing meaningful sound. The technique gives her movement and combat a strong stealth and assassin character.

02 · Visible anomaly

Two points. No disclosed function.

Her horns, dark clothing and two glowing pink points on the forehead are recognized visual details. The points have confirmed significance and appear connected to a form of power.
