Notes

  • Rapax was born from an ordinary ritual made extraordinary by the Air Rune placed within it.
  • His value lies in speed, retrieval, scouting, evasion and returning before opposition can properly respond.
  • He began weaker than Nox in direct combat, but his growth has been unusually rapid.
  • Only one Resonance threshold remains before he may qualify for the ritual that would raise him into Phase II.
  • The question is no longer only what Rapax can collect, but what could ever remain fast enough to keep it from him.
  • His Air Rune did not merely add speed; it shaped the instincts of the servant that emerged.
  • If he reaches Phase II, the result may not simply be a stronger scout, but something harder to keep within reach at all.
