01 · Known discipline

Survival through controlled space.

Yori belonged to Faaram's older servant order. Her work was defensive and spatial rather than a claim to unrelated schools of magic.

Barrier craft

Hold what must endure

Her recorded specialties included barriers, wards, veils, shields and protective domes—methods for controlling sight, access and the shape of a battlefield.

Portal utility

Open what has no path

Yori could create routes through hostile ground. The Archive preserves the function without assigning invented technique names.

02 · Final record

The Abyssal Rift Incident.

Faaram was fleeing Toji and Yuji when he was forced to open the Abyssal Rift. The event destroyed, consumed or pulled in his estate.

Confirmed outcome

Yori died in the Rift.

Yori was lost during the incident. Faaram also lost his body while the escape was being forced open.
