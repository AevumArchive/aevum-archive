Notes

  • Yori specialized in barrier magic rather than direct destructive force.
  • She could raise magical shields and broad dome-like protections around allies or positions.
  • Her veils controlled sight, access and the shape of a battlefield.
  • Portals allowed her to create routes where none existed, making her exceptionally valuable as a utility servant.
  • During the Abyssal Rift Incident, Faaram had to cast the Abyssal Rift to escape Toji and Yuji.
  • Yori died in that incident while the escape was being forced open.

Barrier craft

Shields and protective domes let Yori hold space long enough for others to act.

Veils and portals

She could close information away, control passage and open a route through hostile ground.

Abyssal Rift

Her final record belongs to the escape from Toji and Yuji, when Faaram was forced to open the Rift.
