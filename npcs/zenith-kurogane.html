Spiritual Pressure

Zenith Kurogane possesses an immense amount of Spiritual Power. People feel its pressure the moment they enter his presence, even if he is standing still. Most people below the highest ranks are overwhelmed by that pressure alone.

Loss of consciousness
Some pass out under the weight of his Spiritual Pressure.
Rigid immobility
Others remain conscious but go completely rigid. They are aware of Zenith’s presence and cannot move their bodies under the pressure.

A name that stayed

Kazz called it Spiritual Power in a conversation with Kenji, who had known it by another name. The term fit so well that Kenji and the Tengoku Clan took it up.

The Tengoku clan

An ancient warrior clan renowned for its exceptionally powerful samurai and shinobi. The Tengoku are especially notorious for Kagegane, an alloy whose method of manufacture has been passed down exclusively within the clan for generations.

Kagegane

A jet-black alloy of exceptional durability. The Tengoku use it chiefly for the armor and equipment of their highest-ranking members.
