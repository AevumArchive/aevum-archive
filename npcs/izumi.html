Izumi

The Veiled Divine

A divine-level presence within the Götterfamilie: controlled, unreadable and powerful enough that no witnessed record establishes her upper limit.

01 · Observed

Power without performance.

Izumi does not need spectacle to establish danger. Her calm certainty and dealings with entities such as Bomir place her among the setting's genuinely high-level presences.

I

Commonly observed

Masked / Restricted

Used around those she does not trust—or those she judges too weak to require more. The mask and self-imposed limitation do not make this a weak form.

Glowing green eyes remain visible through its horizontal slits, while the striped trousers and long green hair preserve its deliberately unsettling simplicity.

II

Rarely witnessed

God / Trusted

Revealed only to people she trusts, or when remaining restricted would place her at a serious disadvantage. The form is less concealed—not better understood.

Its bright eyes, dark horn-like features and restrained black clothing communicate power without declaring its source or limit.

Known ability

Reality yields

Izumi can pass directly through solid walls and move through the ground. The record reads less like conventional ghostly movement and more like physical matter failing to restrict her.

Known association

Götterfamilie

The Götterfamilie is an alliance, not a biological family. Izumi and Bomir are not blood-related.

Archive boundary

Unmeasured

No complete ability list, origin, age, exact taxonomy, motive or power ceiling has been witnessed. The Archive assigns no invented Power Level.
