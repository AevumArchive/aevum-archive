I

01 / After Bomir

Free from the Orb.
Between life and death.

Liberation

Foreign control ended.

A mysterious Orb had been implanted in Jiro's chest without his conscious knowledge. It influenced his will for a long time and could grant him additional power. During the final conflict, Bomir forcibly removed it.

Critical injuries

No promise of waking.

Jiro lost his left arm while fighting Bomir and suffered a massive hole in his chest. His condition is critical. No one knows whether he will survive.

Faaram is waiting to learn whether Jiro will ever wake again.

II

02 / The disappearance

He was trying
to warn Faaram.

In the old servant cellar in Tal, Jiro and Ichiro were sharing tea when they noticed someone approaching—possibly several people. Jiro rushed out to warn Faaram.

He never reached him.

Jiro was probably intercepted on the way. He has no memory of how the mysterious Orb was implanted in his chest, and did not consciously know it had been placed there.

The interception is suspected. The implanted Orb and its long influence over his will are confirmed. Earlier signs of familiar speech and recognition did not mean that he remembered what had been done to him.

He did not choose to abandon Faaram. He was trying to reach him.
III

03 / Defining soul trait

Reinforced Soul

Open trait record ↗

In the earlier likeness,
His soul appears to insist that he stands there twice.

Jiro's soul possesses an abnormal resilience that has persisted across death, restoration and violent spiritual disruption. Its exceptional stability has resisted attempts to alter, overwrite or separate it from his body. That resilience did not prevent the implanted Orb from influencing his will.

When its presence becomes visible, Jiro appears to leave behind a second impression of himself: a pale violet echo occupying almost the same place, as though his existence has been reinforced beyond the boundary of a single body.

The echo is Jiro's own soul. Its silhouette follows his posture and carries no separate personality or will.

The phenomenon remains closely associated with his survival, restoration and continued resistance to damage that should have ended an ordinary soul.

V

05 / The truth of the lost years

Not a willing
betrayal.

Both brothers spent a very long time under outside control. Much of what they did during that time was not their own free choice.

Earlier accounts described Jiro's cruelty and contempt as though they offered a clear view of his own will. The Orb's influence means that certainty no longer holds.

Kazz supported Faaram by lending him part of his power. He wanted the control broken and further bloodshed avoided. He did not intend to kill Ichiro or Jiro.

For the first time in a long time, no foreign will stands between the brothers and themselves. What remains is the damage.

VII

07 / Chronicle

What he tried
to return to.

  1. Shared origin

    The older history records the brothers' destroyed home planet and original universe, followed by their restoration through perfect souls. Jiro returned without the old memories.

  2. The right hand

    Jiro became a pillar of Faaram's former order. His Reinforced Soul was known for resisting direct soul manipulation.

  3. The cellar in Tal

    Jiro left the room he shared with Ichiro to warn Faaram of approaching people. He never reached him and was probably intercepted.

  4. The unseen implantation

    Jiro has no memory of the Orb being placed in his chest. It influenced his will for a long time and could lend him additional power.

  5. Earlier returns

    Ultimate Empowered Resurrection is recorded in his earlier history. It cost Faaram the ability itself and a wound that did not heal normally. Jiro survived the Aizen event with severe injuries; he did not die there.

  6. Bomir

    Empowered by the Orb, Jiro injured Bomir. In the same conflict he lost his left arm and suffered a massive chest wound. Bomir tore out the Orb and ended its control. Jiro's survival remains uncertain.

VIII

08 / Unanswered

Will he wake?

Jiro's survival is unknown. The Orb's identity, who implanted it, the full reach of its influence and Jiro's unamplified current strength remain unconfirmed.

The control is over. Recovery has not been promised.
