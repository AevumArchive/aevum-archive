I

01 / Current archive state

The mind remembers.
The power follows.

Memory

Appears restored

Recent interactions suggest that the fragmentation surrounding Jiro's memories has largely disappeared. His speech, recognition and behavior now carry the unsettling consistency of someone who remembers his past in full.

Whether every memory has truly returned cannot yet be proven, but Jiro no longer behaves like a man searching for what was lost.

Power

Not fully restored

Memory has returned faster than power. Whatever restoration has occurred within Jiro's mind and soul, his present strength does not yet appear to equal the full extent of what he once possessed.

Jiro remains extremely dangerous. His historical peak was considerably more dangerous still. The distance between those states remains unresolved.

II

02 / Memory return

The Man
Who Remembers

There was once uncertainty in what Jiro remembered. Pieces of recognition surfaced unevenly. Old bonds carried weight before their meaning was entirely clear, and parts of his past seemed to exist just beyond conscious reach.

That uncertainty is disappearing.

Jiro now reacts to old names, old relationships and old history with a familiarity that no longer feels reconstructed. There is no visible searching behind his answers. No hesitation suggesting that he is rebuilding himself from fragments.

It increasingly feels as though Jiro has recovered his full memory.

His power has not followed at the same pace. The soul remembers what the body cannot yet reproduce.

“Jiro remembers the height from which he once looked down upon the world. He simply has not climbed back to it yet.”
III

03 / Defining soul trait

Reinforced Soul

Open trait record ↗

Jiro stands once.
His soul appears to insist that he stands there twice.

Jiro's soul possesses an abnormal resilience that has persisted across death, restoration and violent spiritual disruption. Its exceptional stability has resisted attempts to alter, overwrite or separate it from his body.

When its presence becomes visible, Jiro appears to leave behind a second impression of himself: a pale violet echo occupying almost the same place, as though his existence has been reinforced beyond the boundary of a single body.

The echo is Jiro's own soul. Its silhouette follows his posture and carries no separate personality or will.

The phenomenon remains closely associated with his survival, restoration and continued resistance to damage that should have ended an ordinary soul.

Controlled force

His recorded fighting style centers on extreme physical strength, speed, flight and aerial control. He needed no dominant weapon or spell circle: displaced air, loose stone and the movement of his coat often announced his ascent. Those historical feats do not establish his present limits.

V

05 / Disposition

A World
Beneath Him

Jiro's contempt for others has become increasingly difficult to mistake for simple arrogance.

He does not appear to divide the world primarily into allies and enemies. He divides it into beings worthy of acknowledgment — and everything else.

Ordinary lives hold almost no inherent value in his worldview. Weakness does not inspire sympathy. Fear does not earn mercy. Numbers do not impress him.

In many situations, killing something beneath him would require more interest than ignoring it. His cruelty is carried by indifference and superiority, rather than constant violence.

Most living things are not opponents to Jiro. They are vermin that happen to occupy the same world.

A Place Never Easily Replaced

Jiro once stood at Faaram's right hand, occupying a place within his servant order that was never easily replaced. His loyalty was conscious and chosen.

Faaram once restored him through Ultimate Empowered Resurrection. The act cost Faaram the ability itself and left a wound that did not close normally.

VII

07 / Chronicle

What endured.

  1. Shared origin

    Jiro and Ichiro destroyed their home planet and eventually their original universe before powers strong enough to end them intervened. Faaram later restored or recreated both through perfect souls. Jiro returned without the old memories; Ichiro remembered everything.

  2. The right hand

    Jiro became one of the strongest pillars of Faaram's former order. His Reinforced Soul resisted Ichiro's direct control.

  3. Memories forced open

    During his betrayal, Ichiro restored Jiro's lost memories. Jiro was overwhelmed and stood apart from the final confrontation. Ichiro was killed through the Death Note; Keanu later sealed his remains in the Shadow Realm.

  4. The price of restoration

    An earlier death led Faaram to use Ultimate Empowered Resurrection. The return cost Faaram the resurrection ability and a wound that would not heal normally.

  5. The Aizen event

    Jiro survived the Aizen event with severe injuries.

  6. Seen again

    Jiro is alive and recovering. Recent interactions suggest his memories have largely returned, while his former peak strength remains unrestored. The exact current Power Level is unconfirmed.

VIII

08 / Unresolved records

What remains beyond reach.

The complete extent of Jiro's returned memory remains unverified. His current measured strength, the distance to his former peak and the deeper limits of Reinforced Soul remain unresolved.
