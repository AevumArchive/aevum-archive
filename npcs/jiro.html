I

01 / After the awakening

Free. Awake.
Stronger than before.

Awakening

A new threshold.

Jiro has healed from the critical aftermath and awakened through training with Ichiro. He has reached a new level of strength: 3000 PL.

A mysterious Orb had been implanted in Jiro's chest without his conscious knowledge. It influenced his will for a long time and could grant him additional power. During the final conflict, Bomir forcibly removed it.

Lasting injury

Permanent damage.

His left arm is permanently injured. His left hand requires special protection even though he has otherwise healed.

During the fight with Bomir, Jiro lost his left arm and suffered a massive hole in his chest. His condition was critical and his survival was uncertain. That immediate aftermath is now history; the permanent injury remains part of his current condition.

Poison had held Jiro back to keep him from attacking Faaram alongside Ichiro. When Jiro nearly reached Faaram later in the confrontation, Bomir used an unnamed ability to pull him away and into their own fight.

Jiro is now pursuing the sword inherited through his family for generations.

II

02 / The disappearance

He was trying
to warn Faaram.

In the old servant cellar in Tal, Jiro and Ichiro were sharing tea when they noticed someone approaching—possibly several people. Jiro rushed out to warn Faaram.

He never reached him.

Jiro was probably intercepted on the way. He has no memory of how the mysterious Orb was implanted in his chest, and did not consciously know it had been placed there.

The interception is suspected. The implanted Orb and its long influence over his will are confirmed. Earlier signs of familiar speech and recognition did not mean that he remembered what had been done to him.

He did not choose to abandon Faaram. He was trying to reach him.
III

03 / Defining soul trait

Reinforced Soul

Open trait record ↗

In the earlier likeness,
His soul appears to insist that he stands there twice.

Jiro's soul possesses an abnormal resilience that has persisted across death, restoration and violent spiritual disruption. Its exceptional stability has resisted attempts to alter, overwrite or separate it from his body. That resilience did not prevent the implanted Orb from influencing his will.

When its presence becomes visible, Jiro appears to leave behind a second impression of himself: a pale violet echo occupying almost the same place, as though his existence has been reinforced beyond the boundary of a single body.

The echo is Jiro's own soul. Its silhouette follows his posture and carries no separate personality or will.

The phenomenon remains closely associated with his survival, restoration and continued resistance to damage that should have ended an ordinary soul.

The current 3000 PL follows Jiro's awakening through training with Ichiro. The earlier 2200–2400 range belongs to a previous record; it is not his current Power Level.

Enough to wound Bomir

The Orb's additional power was substantial: Jiro was able to injure Bomir during their fight. Its precise amplification during that battle remains unknown. Jiro's later training awakening establishes his current 3000 PL.

Bomir felt that Jiro could genuinely kill him and used Divinity Beyond Death. Its exact effect is unknown. Jiro then endured 30 uninterrupted seconds of Bomir's blows—estimated at a little over 200 hits. Each impact was audible across roughly half the planet; vibrations travelled hundreds of kilometres.

Those thirty seconds describe the barrage, not a confirmed ability duration. The current 3000 PL comes from the later training awakening, not from a calculation of those impacts.

His earlier fighting record centers on extreme physical strength, speed, flight and aerial control. His new strength does not erase the permanent injury to his left arm or the need to protect his left hand.

VI

06 / The truth of the lost years

Not a willing
betrayal.

Both brothers spent a very long time under outside control. Much of what they did during that time was not their own free choice.

Earlier accounts described Jiro's cruelty and contempt as though they offered a clear view of his own will. The Orb's influence means that certainty no longer holds.

Kazz supported Faaram by lending him part of his power. He wanted the control broken and further bloodshed avoided. He did not intend to kill Ichiro or Jiro.

For the first time in a long time, no foreign will stands between the brothers and themselves. What remains is the damage.

VIII

08 / Chronicle

What he tried
to return to.

  1. Shared origin

    The older history records the brothers' destroyed home planet and original universe, followed by their restoration through perfect souls. Jiro returned without the old memories.

  2. The right hand

    Jiro became a pillar of Faaram's former order. His Reinforced Soul was known for resisting direct soul manipulation.

  3. The cellar in Tal

    Jiro left the room he shared with Ichiro to warn Faaram of approaching people. He never reached him and was probably intercepted.

  4. The unseen implantation

    Jiro has no memory of the Orb being placed in his chest. It influenced his will for a long time and could lend him additional power.

  5. Earlier returns

    Ultimate Empowered Resurrection is recorded in his earlier history. It cost Faaram the ability itself and a wound that did not heal normally. Jiro survived the Aizen event with severe injuries; he did not die there.

  6. Bomir

    Empowered by the Orb, Jiro injured Bomir. In the same conflict he lost his left arm and suffered a massive chest wound. Bomir tore out the Orb and ended its control. His survival was uncertain in the immediate aftermath.

  7. Awakening with Ichiro

    Jiro has healed. Training with Ichiro awakened further strength and brought him to 3000 PL. His left arm remains permanently injured, and his left hand requires special protection.

  8. The family sword

    He is searching for the sword passed down through his family for generations.

IX

09 / Unanswered

A sword still missing.
Questions still open.

Jiro is awake and healed, with an awakened Power Level of 3000. His family's inherited sword has not yet been found. Its name, whereabouts and properties are not recorded.

The Orb's identity, who implanted it and the full reach of its influence remain unknown. Faaram can no longer sense Poison after she held Jiro back; she may have died during that intervention, but her fate remains unconfirmed.
