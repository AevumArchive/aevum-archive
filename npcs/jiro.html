Notes

  • Jiro was one of Faaram's self-made servants and one of the strongest pillars of his former order.
  • His loyalty was conscious and chosen. He stood beside Faaram because he decided that was where he belonged, not because he lacked a will of his own.
  • He carried an almost perfected fighter's physique: overwhelming strength and extreme speed held behind calm confidence rather than rage.
  • His strength lived in direct violence: flight, brutal impact, aerial control and the power to tear threats from the sky. Jiro himself was the weapon.
  • His flight showed no wings or visible mechanism. Displaced air, loose stone and the movement of his coat were often the only warning that he was about to rise.
  • His Reinforced Soul was extraordinarily stable and resisted attempts to alter, overwrite or separate it from his body.
  • In rare moments, Jiro's Reinforced Soul became visible as a pale white-violet echo held almost perfectly within his outline — body and soul anchored so firmly together that neither could be pulled from the other.
  • Jiro once died and was later restored by Faaram through Ultimate Empowered Resurrection, a return that cost Faaram the ability itself and left a wound that did not close normally.
  • After the Aizen event, Jiro's record is no longer active. His return remains remembered, but the hand has fallen again.

Controlled force

Jiro needed no dominant weapon or spell circle. Pressure, speed and physical impact made the environment react before he struck.

Reinforced Soul

Its strength was stability. The closer body and visible soul remained aligned, the clearer the truth became: neither was easily rewritten or pulled apart.

Current echo

Jiro's fall after return leaves an empty place in the servant web, one that cannot be filled by ordinary summoned hands.
