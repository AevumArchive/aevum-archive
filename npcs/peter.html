01 · Two names, one existence

The White beneath the skin.

Peter and Blanc are not rival beings. They are the life built beneath the seal and the ancient existence that survived behind it.

Long before the name Peter belonged to him, he was known as Blanc—a Primal Demon of the First Color White. The First Colors are neither ranks nor ceremonial titles. White is not an affinity Peter learned; it is part of what he is.

That nature sometimes forces itself through his present form as an unnatural white manifestation across the face and around one eye. It is not summoned like a spell. It escapes, briefly thinning the boundary between Peter and Blanc.

The Archive does not know how far the manifestation can spread, nor what would remain visible if the boundary disappeared entirely.

02 · The seal

Buried. Broken. Becoming.

Before

Buried memory

The seal did more than restrain Peter's strength. It removed his knowledge of Blanc, his Primal nature and much of the power connected to it.

Fracture

The seal broke

His memories returned. The strength suppressed within the seal returned with them. Peter awakened knowing what had existed before his current name.

Now

Power without mastery

Recovered power is not mastered power. He continues to adapt as body and mind learn to contain what had remained silent for too long.

03 · The right hand

He was never expendable.

Peter's place beside OBAMA cannot be reduced to command. He remained through lost power, death, changed bodies and the return of authority. When Peter stood at the edge of death, OBAMA chose him and paid with his own life.

Peter was never blamed for that choice. No resentment followed. For OBAMA, saving him had been worth the price.

05 · Absolute restriction

The Dragon Emblem

A Dragon Emblem appears upon Peter's clothing. Its meaning belongs to Peter alone. No scholar, demon, god, Higher Being or other known existence shares that knowledge.

The Archive preserves only the consequence: if the meaning were revealed, an unknown entity would answer with total erasure. Existence would begin again from the Fracture of the Old World, but the same history would not return.

The symbol may be witnessed. Its meaning must never be spoken.

Present state

Peter lives. Blanc remembers.

The seal is gone, but Peter has not discarded the identity formed beneath it. He now carries both the ancient being that awakened and the experiences that made him Peter. His power has returned. Its final shape has not.
