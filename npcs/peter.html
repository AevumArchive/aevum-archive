Notes

  • Peter entered OBAMA's service through Death and became the companion around whom OBAMA's deepest loyalty formed.
  • OBAMA once chose Peter over obedience to Death and later sacrificed himself for him.
  • Peter now bears one of OBAMA's Mandates as a living bond rather than a simple command.
  • His memory and power were deliberately sealed for a long time, leaving him unaware of the full shape of his origin.
  • That seal has been broken. Peter's full strength has returned, though he is still learning how to exist with it.
  • A dragon emblem is carried on the back of his jacket. Its meaning is forbidden knowledge and is not opened in the archive.

Loyalty

Peter is not merely a servant record. His bond changed the choices, death and return of OBAMA himself.

Awakening

The seal over his memory and strength has broken. What returned is complete, but not yet familiar to him.

The dragon mark

The emblem is visible. Its meaning is not spoken, explained or opened by this archive.
