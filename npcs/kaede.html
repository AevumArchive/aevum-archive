On the road

Kaede usually looks a little short on sleep. She speaks plainly, has a dry sense of humor and rarely gives a long explanation when a short one will do. She keeps her distance at first. Trust takes more than one encounter.

Bring her a relic she has never seen, or show her a ruin that appears on none of the old maps, and her attention sharpens. She can joke her way through danger, then go quiet when a detail seems familiar.

Unexplained phenomenon

Continuity Scar

Sometimes a face, a name or a whole conversation feels familiar to Kaede even though it never happened in this world. She may remember a place before it existed or recall an event ending differently.

She suspects the fragments come from another world or timeline. Their source is still unknown. A fragment rarely gives her enough to tell what happened before it or what will follow.

Ability

Echo Recall

Kaede can perceive traces of events attached to places, relics, ordinary objects, souls and powerful anomalies. She calls them Echoes.

An Echo might show an actual event, a possible future or a different version of what happened. Others are distorted or incomplete. Kaede can use them to follow a lead, though an Echo can send her in the wrong direction.

Weapon

Afterimage

An old one-handed blade that can preserve an echo of Kaede’s movements. A slash can repeat seconds later from the spot where she first made it, even after she has moved away.

Passive

Déjà Vu

Kaede sometimes gets the distinct feeling that she has already seen the next movement. Repeated techniques make these impressions more reliable, and occasionally she gets one for an attack she has never faced.

Kaede’s journal

The Same Book

“If you're reading this again, then I was right.”

Kaede keeps a small black journal of places, people, relics and memories she cannot explain. She writes down the details whenever two accounts of history disagree. Some entries are in her own hand, though she has no memory of writing them.
