Opened testimony

The Sovereign

  • The Bridge is not merely a place Lilith occupies. Its known structure and authority are bound to her rule.
  • The realm functions as an enormous portal hub connecting distant worlds and spaces through major gates and scattered access points.
  • Its outer reaches are hostile. Weak souls that remain too long may be warped into demonic forms and lose what they once were.
  • Ensky has challenged and antagonized Lilith repeatedly. No witnessed attempt has inflicted meaningful damage on her.
  • Lilith speaks with direct, almost casual confidence. She does not need ceremony to make authority obvious.

Archive boundary

Power without measure

The Archive does not attempt to assign a ceiling to Lilith's strength. The witnessed gap between ordinary beings and Lilith is already sufficient.

Only her Demon Form has been directly witnessed. Two further manifestations are known to exist, but their appearance, nature and capabilities remain sealed.
