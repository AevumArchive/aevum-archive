01 · Witnessed abilities

A body that becomes the escape.

Arisu's complete capabilities remain unknown. These techniques are witnessed facts rather than estimates of her full limits.

I

Evasion

Spider Swarm Evasion

When attacked, Arisu can break her physical form apart into a swarm of small spiders, allowing attacks aimed at her body to miss or pass through the swarm. She can then reconstruct herself from those spiders.

This is not ordinary teleportation. Her body physically disperses before reforming.

II

Silent traversal

Silent Surface Movement

Arisu can cling to walls and ceilings with her feet and move across them freely without producing meaningful sound, giving her movement and combat a strongly stealth-oriented character.
