Keanu

The Living Curse

Keanu was not a human covered in shadow. He was a Curse that learned to hold a humanoid shape — Faaram's shadow rather than his blade.

Notes

  • Keanu was remembered as Faaram's shadow: quiet, patient and horrifyingly difficult to remove.
  • He was not a person transformed by a curse. The humanoid figure was a temporary arrangement of dense shadow, smoke, Curse energy and clothing.
  • No ordinary face waited beneath the darkness. Only two narrow points of pale light remained steady while his head and shoulders dissolved into smoke.
  • Thin pale bands around his forearms were among the few fixed marks in a silhouette whose hands, robes and edges never stayed entirely solid.
  • Where Jiro crushed what stood before him, Keanu attached himself to a target — watching, following and appearing only when necessary.
  • He entered the Shadow Realm without a portal by letting his connection to the physical world fall away until even his eyes disappeared.
  • Inside that realm, damaged shadow and Curse matter could gather again. Destroying the visible body was not always the same as destroying Keanu.
  • He could create powerful Curses and heal other Curse entities, with shapes sometimes surfacing briefly inside the darkness around him.
  • After Ichiro fell, Keanu carried the remains into the Shadow Realm and sacrificed his conscious existence to maintain the Curse sealing them there. His later reappearance made the meaning of his disappearance uncertain.
  • Faaram eventually stopped searching for him. The archive records Keanu as lost beyond recovery unless a future age proves otherwise.

Curse shape

Curse Vessel, Living Curse, Shadow Curse, Undying Hex Servant — later names for the same old truth: Keanu was a Curse maintaining a body-shaped shadow.

Shadow retreat

The Shadow Realm made him terrifyingly hard to end. Killing him was not the same as keeping him gone.

Current echo

No grave holds him, no confirmed body remains, and no active search continues. Only the shape of his absence is certain.
