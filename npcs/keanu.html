Notes

  • Keanu was remembered as Faaram's shadow: quiet, patient and horrifyingly difficult to remove.
  • Where Jiro crushed what stood before him, Keanu lingered around a target until the curse became unavoidable.
  • He could enter the Shadow Realm, weave powerful curses and heal curses with equal mastery.
  • Death did not settle easily on him. If destroyed, he could retreat into shadow, regenerate and return.
  • His danger was never measured by raw power alone. His true threat came from persistence, hidden movement and the slow ruin of enemies who thought the fight was over.
  • Faaram eventually stopped searching for him. The archive records Keanu as lost beyond recovery unless a future age proves otherwise.

Curse shape

Curse Vessel, Living Curse, Shadow Curse, Undying Hex Servant — later names for the same old truth: Keanu was a curse with a body.

Shadow retreat

The Shadow Realm made him terrifyingly hard to end. Killing him was not the same as keeping him gone.

Current echo

No grave holds him, no confirmed body remains, and no active search continues. Only the shape of his absence is certain.
