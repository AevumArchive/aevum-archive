Gandred

Gandred is an elderly mage whose search for magic took him far beyond his homeland.

✦

Archive note

Over 3,000 years?

He appears as an elderly man and is believed to be more than 3,000 years old.

01Studies and travels 02Unknown limits

Early mastery

Beyond his homeland.

Gandred mastered most of the spells known in his homeland while he was still young. He left to seek out magic he had yet to learn. Accounts say he offered help to those whose potential caught his attention.

Magical power

A mage without a known ceiling.

What magic Gandred wields and how far it reaches remain a mystery. Few mages who ever lived are thought to rival him.
