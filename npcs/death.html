Since the Beginning

Death predates the recorded histories of civilizations and mortal beings. He and God have existed alongside one another since the beginning of time. Death cannot recall which of them was first, and the circumstances of his origin are unknown.

His primordial nature is inseparable from mortality itself. His record begins before the histories of those who gained power through training, ascension or inheritance.

Death and Mortality

Death embodies both the end of life and the mortality that attends it. The Archive records this as his nature. Its records of necromancy, soul manipulation and physical destruction describe separate practices or events. The ways his primordial power manifests are largely undocumented.

The Pale Horseman

Death is the oldest and strongest of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. The other three names are preserved in tradition. Their activities, whereabouts and involvement in Aevum remain undocumented.

The Former Master

OBAMA began as a human. He became Death’s student before rising into a Chaos Higherbeing. Their relationship belongs to that earlier period, before OBAMA’s current Path of the New Order.

Peter was placed with OBAMA through Death. Over time, OBAMA’s loyalty to Peter became stronger than his obedience to his former master. The teachings Death passed on, the details of their arrangement and how it ended remain undocumented.

Beyond Current Assessment

Death’s strength among the Four Horsemen is established. The Archive has no measurement of his primordial power or its upper limit.
