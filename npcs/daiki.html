The price of his power

Daiki wanted to take his curse power further, so he put a curse on himself. He has been unable to speak since.

Where he came from remains a mystery. Even when he has something to say, it can be hard to tell what he means.

Command without a voice

Daiki belongs to the Tengoku Clan outside the Kurogane family. Like Kenji, he leads a unit of his own.

He communicates entirely through hand signs and movements of his eyes. Sometimes even his own unit is left guessing what he meant.
