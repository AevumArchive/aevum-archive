I

01 / The threat

Archive summary

A threat surviving every answer.

Chax is not remembered for conquest, rule or necessity. The opened testimony records an entity powerful enough to kill gods for amusement and remain beyond permanent elimination.

Known threat

Even Higher Beings failed to make the end permanent.

Attempts to eliminate Chax have not produced a lasting conclusion. The record does not assign him a numerical ceiling, species or catalogue of victims.

03 / The sealed location

Current imprisonment

A sealed location without a map.

Chax remains imprisoned on an unknown planet. The Archive preserves the absence of coordinates; naming a world would create certainty that has not been witnessed.

Sirius Galaxy

A dangerous region holding something worse.

Sirius is regarded as one of the most dangerous galaxies in the universe. Chax's prison exists somewhere within it, hidden among worlds the record does not safely distinguish.

Archive warning
