I

01 / Adaptation

01 · Archive summary

Danger through understanding.

Poison is a former servant of Faaram and one of the more unusual entities among his former servants. Unlike many of his servants, she was not defined purely by overwhelming force.

Her threat lay in the ability to observe a situation, adapt to it and seemingly replicate what she learned. The longer an encounter continued, the more dangerous the encounter could become.

⇄

Known ability

Adaptive Copy

The Archive cannot yet determine whether Poison copies techniques, physical traits, supernatural abilities or something deeper. Its full limits remain unresolved.

II

02 / The final stand

The Overlord finale

She kept the second
fight away from him.

The report about UWU

Before Faaram's final fight with Ichiro, Poison told him she had found UWU. UWU appeared to be on the verge of death. The still-controlled Ichiro had apparently discarded her after his reawakening, seeing no further use for her.

No later recovery or rescue has been confirmed.

Holding Jiro back

Poison stopped Jiro from joining Ichiro against Faaram. Her intervention kept Faaram from having to face both brothers at once.

After the fighting ended, Faaram could no longer sense her. That absence leaves open the possibility that she died trying to hold Jiro back. It does not confirm her death.

04 / Current presence

03 · Present record

The presence he can no longer feel.

Personality

Cold, distant and difficult to read. Poison rarely needs dramatic aggression to appear threatening; the danger lies in the impression that she is always observing and waiting.

Current state

Her earlier death in Satoru Gojo’s Domain is historical; an Event Wish later revived her. Before the final confrontation, she was alive and helping Faaram. Now Faaram can no longer sense Poison. She may have died holding Jiro back, but her death is not confirmed.

Unresolved

Her origins, the complete nature of Adaptive Copy, the true relationship between her forms and the height of her power all remain unwritten.
