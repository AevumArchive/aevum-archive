01 · Archive summary

Danger through understanding.

Poison is a former servant of Faaram and one of the more unusual entities to have served under the Faceless. Unlike many of his servants, she was not defined purely by overwhelming force.

Her threat lay in the ability to observe a situation, adapt to it and seemingly replicate what she learned. The longer an encounter continued, the more dangerous the encounter could become.

⇄

Known ability

Adaptive Copy

The Archive cannot yet determine whether Poison copies techniques, physical traits, supernatural abilities or something deeper. Its full limits remain unresolved.

02 · Manifestations

Two forms. One watcher.

Both manifestations retain the black hair, white horns, dark clothing and stark white tie associated with Poison.

I

Primary manifestation

Female Form

This is the form most strongly associated with Poison and the one she appears most comfortable using. It is believed to carry greater overall combat potential, although the source and exact extent of that advantage remain unknown.

WeaponAzure ScytheBlue, cyan and violet energy
II

Alternative manifestation

Male Form

The male form presents a harsher demeanor—more visibly pessimistic, irritated and hostile. It remains extremely dangerous, but is currently believed to be somewhat more limited than the female manifestation.

03 · Present record

A quiet place after conflict.

Personality

Cold, distant and difficult to read. Poison rarely needs dramatic aggression to appear threatening; the danger lies in the impression that she is always observing and waiting.

Afterlife

Poison is no longer an active servant fighting beside Faaram. Her current representation belongs to an unusually quiet and empty Afterlife rather than an active battlefield.

Unresolved

Her origins, the complete nature of Adaptive Copy, the true relationship between her forms and the height of her power all remain unwritten.
