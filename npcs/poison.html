Poison

A powerful servant of Faaram whose two embodied aspects could change both personality and weapon without diminishing the danger she carried.

Notes

  • Poison's original form is female, but she can deliberately shift into a male counterpart with a distinct personality.
  • Her female aspect fights with a scythe. Her male aspect carries a saber.
  • Seeing a person or NPC once is enough for Poison to reproduce that being's power and meet the strength she observed.
  • The copied strength made her one of Faaram's most dangerous servants and allowed her to help him through threats ordinary summoned hands could not answer.
  • Poison died in battle against Satoru Gojo when his Domain overcame her.
  • Her death occurred before the Abyssal Rift Incident.

Mirrored selves

Her transformation changes personality and weapon: scythe in the female aspect, saber in the male aspect.

Observed power

One clear sight of another being is enough for Poison to copy its power and rise to the same strength.

Last record

Her path ended inside Satoru Gojo's Domain, before the Abyssal Rift Incident claimed another servant.
