Spiritual Pressure

The full extent of Kiyoko’s power remains unknown. She needs a Stillweave blindfold to keep the Spiritual Pressure she gives off under control.

If that pressure were free to radiate into her surroundings, almost nobody could endure being near her.

Stillweave

A rare spiritual fabric woven to contain overwhelming Spiritual Pressure. It forces the wearer’s pressure to stay compressed close to the body, limiting how far it radiates into the surroundings.

Kiyoko’s blindfold is made from this fabric. It keeps her pressure close enough to her that others can be in her presence.
