Confirmed background

From Sundara to Avalon.

  • Born in a small village in the Kingdom of Sundara.
  • Worked as an adventurer long ago; the rest of that history is unopened.
  • Began referencing an early adventurer guild, but did not finish the account.
  • Came to Avalon, chose to remain and appears to have built his reputation himself.

Present responsibility

Protection beyond one village.

Raoul works for the Kingdom of Avalon and describes responsibilities involving the safety of certain villages and regions. He is not an ordinary village guard, but the Archive assigns him no military rank, noble title, secret office or command position.

His duties keep him away from direct front-line fighting. He nevertheless said he would offer his sword if Avalon's King personally demanded it.

Crownbridge work

People, depots and deliveries.

Raoul has influential contacts, can acquire materials and organize deliveries, and communicates with people working around Crownbridge. He refers to some as “my people.” He planned to return toward Crownbridge around 09:00 and report Lythariel's humanitarian agreement.

William's supplies will be collected through an intermediate depot after roughly one or two days, confirming organized wartime transport rather than instant movement.

Unidentified patronage

The Grand Duke's purse.

Raoul expects Lythariel's materials to be paid as wartime procurement from an unidentified Grand Duke's considerable wealth. His casual remarks imply access or authorization, but do not establish friendship, dislike, office or the Grand Duke's identity.

Lythariel

He accepted her refusal to produce harmful alchemy and will bring her list of materials directly to her secluded home. She will provide healing and humanitarian supplies only.

Elias and William

Raoul was a very close friend of Elias and knew the old tavern and cellar. Elias spoke to him about William, but Raoul did not personally meet William until after Elias died and William inherited the tavern.

Intentionally unresolved

Authority without a named rank.

Raoul's exact rank, office, relationship to the Grand Duke, the Grand Duke's identity and the remainder of Raoul's adventuring history remain unknown.
