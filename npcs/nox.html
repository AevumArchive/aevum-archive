Notes

  • Nox was not made to replace Jiro, Keanu or any lost pillar. He became the first foundation of what Faaram intended to build next.
  • His strength lies in adaptation, combat growth and the Resonance bond that deepens through service.
  • Early records placed him in Phase I near Power Level 280 before Ascension reinforced him into Phase II.
  • He has gathered ritual materials, secured mana potions and assisted with preparations tied to Faaram's ritual work.
  • Within Faaram's new order, Nox is the blade still being forged.
  • His existence proves that Faaram's servants are no longer fixed at creation; they can change, deepen and ascend.
  • The Grimoire of Remnant Thrones marks the era he belongs to: remnants gathered into a new structure.

Phase

Phase II, Resonance I. His first Ascension has already been survived.

Shape

Adaptive combat vessel, not a finished weapon.

Known tie

Associated with the Grimoire of Remnant Thrones and Faaram's modern servant system.
