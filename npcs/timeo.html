Notes

  • Timeo carries two separate Treacherous Cleavers. Each is a World Item; their individual effects remain unrecorded. Open World Item record.

  • Timeo's earlier record belongs to Faaram's servant timeline and the lost years of the brothers' foreign control.
  • The record does not claim that history caused the Rampage Form. Its origin and ending remain unopened.
