I / Across worlds

A different life.
The same name.

This is the Faaram once witnessed in another multiverse as it began to collapse. He has since crossed into our multiverse and made his way to Universe 2.

He has never died. His power was earned through his own abilities, experience, development and Servant Arts, shaped by the challenges he survived.

His history is separate from Faaram Tempest.

II / Servant Arts

Every bond.
One sovereign.

Control, stability and resonance. His command reaches across the enduring connections between himself and his Servants.

01 / Signature

Sovereign of
the Bound

Faaram has extraordinary control over permanently bound Servants. He strengthens their Bonds, raises their stability and coordinates multiple connections at once, deliberately shaping the resonance between himself and each Servant.

Even extremely powerful Servants can be commanded simultaneously without destabilizing his control.

02 / Servant Art

Servant Art — Sovereign Form

The highest known evolution of his Servant techniques. Faaram strengthens several Servants at once, sustains them across extreme distances and coordinates complex commands with almost no delay.

He can share the perception of multiple Servants in parallel, receiving information through their senses.

03 / Servant Ability

Borrowed Throne

For a short time, Faaram can wield a characteristic ability of a permanently bound Servant himself. The Servant does not need to be present or summoned.

The ability draws on Faaram’s own stats, resources and Magical Power. In his hands, its strength or application may differ significantly from the original.

III / Soul Arts · 04 / Ultimate Soul Art

Soul
Dominion

Faaram can directly grasp and immobilize souls, separate them from one another, stabilize damaged souls or forcibly tear away parts of a soul.

Exceptionally powerful or heavily protected souls can resist. Ordinary Soul Resistance is usually insufficient to fully withstand his control.
✧Within his reach

05 / Ability

Severance

He strikes at a connection. A Summoner and their Summon. A Mage and a Barrier. A Soul and its Binding. A Relic and its User. A Caster and an active Buff.

Weaker connections can be severed immediately. Extremely powerful bonds must first be weakened, or broken through several successful applications.

06 / Soul Art

Soul Imprint

Through direct physical contact, Faaram reads the condition of a soul. Damage, active Bindings, artificial alterations, unstable power structures, strong outside influences and deeply defining traces of past events can reveal themselves.

Soul Imprint does not read thoughts or grant full access to memories.

07 / Forbidden Art

Soulforge

Damaged Soul Fragments can be stabilized, joined, strengthened or integrated into suitable Vessels. The more severe the damage, or the more incompatible the fragments, the more difficult and dangerous the process becomes.

Restoration is not instantaneous. Extremely damaged souls may require preparation.

08 / Defensive Passive

Unbroken Soul

Intensive training, Soul Arts, extreme strain and countless survived conflicts have given his soul extraordinary stability.

  • Soul Destruction
  • Possession
  • Soul Displacement
  • Forced Soul Extraction
  • Foreign Soul Control
Massive resistance. Never complete immunity.

IV / 09 / Spell

Black Star.

Enormous quantities of his own Magical Power compress into a tiny, profoundly unstable core. Its pull drags in opponents, projectiles and magical constructs.

Then the condensed energy collapses inward, striking everything within its reach with extreme compression.

Force, radius and the duration of the pull scale with the energy Faaram invests.

V / Lasting strength

Nothing endured
leaves him unchanged.

Experience becomes understanding. Challenges become growth. Long-held bonds leave their resonance within him.

10 / Passive

Unbroken Continuity

Every significant battle, powerful opponent survived and extraordinary experience leaves a lasting mark on his development. Great challenges become a permanent part of his strength.

Extreme situations can refine abilities, increase resistances, evolve existing techniques or raise his overall power. Greater challenges and more meaningful experiences offer greater potential for permanent growth.

11 / Passive

Thousand-Year Echo

Every serious fight deepens his combat experience. Repeated movements, spell sequences, abilities and tactical patterns become increasingly readable, predictable and vulnerable to his counters.

He adapts to the opponent throughout a battle. This ability does not copy techniques.

12 / Aura / Passive

Bound Resonance

Servants bound to Faaram over long periods leave a resonance within his Soul and Servant Arts. That imprint does not disappear completely when a Servant is no longer active at his side.

The strength, duration and significance of each bond determine its lasting resonance, and how much it can strengthen his Soul and Servant techniques over time.
