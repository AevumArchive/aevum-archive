Current NPC record · Identified visitor

Arnold.

The previously unnamed visitor to Lythariel's home: a familiar acquaintance, a wary traveler and one of the builders of the Chapel of the First Light.

LytharielAvalon
☀

Known work

First Light

Arnold helped construct the chapel in Avalon's capital.

Home visit

Rare plants and unfamiliar companions.

Arnold already knew Lythariel. At her secluded property he admired the view, the scale of the land and the house she built herself. He noticed rare plants and accepted her warning that some should not be touched.

He had heard of Theodor but apparently had not seen him before, and he was introduced to Tehla during the visit.

Road observations

Bolder bandits.

Arnold observed that bandits are becoming increasingly bold and suggested the war may be contributing. He prefers walking to riding. He can swim, but prefers not to be in water.

Archive distinction

Arnold is not Raoul.

Arnold's home visit and chapel connection are separate from Raoul's Avalon duties, Grand Duke financing and Crownbridge logistics.
