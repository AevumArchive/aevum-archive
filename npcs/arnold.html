First meeting

Rare plants and unfamiliar companions.

Arnold met Lythariel for the first time during this visit to her secluded property. He also helped with its interior furnishing. He admired the view, the scale of the land and the house she built herself. He noticed rare plants and accepted her warning that some should not be touched.

He had heard of Theodor but apparently had not seen him before, and he was introduced to Tehla during the visit.

Road observations

Bolder bandits.

Arnold observed that bandits are becoming increasingly bold and suggested the war may be contributing. He prefers walking to riding. He can swim, but prefers not to be in water.
