Archive notes

  • Veyr exists because something in the world was placed incorrectly. The damaged World Anchor did not produce an ordinary wound; it produced a person-shaped error.
  • He appears aware that something is wrong beneath the ground and around the Anchor, though he speaks through riddles rather than direct instruction.
  • During The Silent Fault, his clues repeatedly circled roots, weight and the thing that holds a world in place.
  • The answer to his repeated riddle was “Anchor.” His guidance was useful, but never clean enough to make his motives certain.
  • Veyr is not recorded as purely hostile. That does not make him safe.
