Kazz · Spirit Realm

His body fell. His soul endured.

The Faceless killed Kazz’s body. His soul survived in the Spirit Realm, and his grudge survived with it. Kazz later helped Faaram Tempest against Ichiro to repay a debt of honor. In Kazz’s eyes, Tempest had put an end to the Faceless.

Faaram Tempest · Separate identity

His own body. His own soul.

Faaram Tempest is a different person with a life of his own. He has never lost his soul or changed bodies. The Faceless belongs to the past.
