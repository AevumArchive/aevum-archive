TheFaceless

When the Faceless took hold of Faaram, it was trying to make itself fully real. The body it used is gone. Whether the thing inside it can die remains unknown.

Host destroyed · Entity’s fate unknown

Kazz · Spirit Realm

His body fell. His soul endured.

The Faceless killed Kazz’s body. His soul survived in the Spirit Realm, and his grudge survived with it. Kazz later helped Faaram Tempest against Ichiro to repay a debt of honor. In Kazz’s eyes, Tempest had put an end to the Faceless.

The earlier Faaram · Possession

It wanted to exist.

The Faceless had a will of its own. It possessed the earlier Faaram and meant to use him as its way into the world. Faaram Tempest is a separate person who has always had his own body and soul.
