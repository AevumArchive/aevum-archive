Starting a character

The public foundation.

Character identity and progression stay separate.

Character Sheet

Record race, class, subclass, advanced class, traits, path and known equipment only where they apply.

Power Level

PL is a measured power value. It is not Class Mastery and must not be treated as the same scale.

Class Mastery

Mastery tracks development within a class. It does not replace Power Level.

Combat and adventure

Systems on the road.

Only opened behavior is summarized.

Mana and Combat Mode

Ordinary spells use Mana. Combat Mode supports quickslots, reactions, conditions, barriers, shields, Counterspell and Metamagic.

Rest, Camps and Inns

Recovery and travel use different public contexts; exact private administration remains outside this handbook.

Quests and World Level

Quests, discoveries and World Level shape the public adventure record without exposing GM controls.

Supernatural boundaries

  • Summons, transformations and forms remain distinct concepts.
  • Restricted Magic, Super-Tier Magic and World-Class Magic remain separate.
  • Archmage does not bypass Restricted Magic.
  • Character-bound Unique and Ultimate authorities remain character-bound.
  • Faaram's servant mechanics are not generic summoning rules.
