When the Lords answer.

At a certain point in the realm's progression, major regional bosses begin to emerge. These Lords form the road toward something greater: the Chapter Boss at the end of an age.

Lords are not Chapter Bosses.

Lords are major encounters that form the progression gate. A Chapter Boss is the culmination of an entire chapter and a substantially greater milestone.

Chapter Boss milestone

Not merely another boss kill.

Defeating a Chapter Boss marks a major progression breakpoint. The first opens Chapter II with a very large PlayerPoints reward, valuable progression items, new systems and substantial new content. The second opens Chapter III with the same narrative weight: the end of one chapter and the beginning of another.

A deeper archive

Three victories leave three traces.

After each completed Chapter Boss, a fragment of an Archive cipher may be found. Assemble all three fragments to open records that do not belong on the public shelves.
