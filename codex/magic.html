Power has cost and condition.

A high-level public explanation of the spell direction. It does not list private admin abilities or sealed vault mechanics.

Resource

Mana first.

AuraSkills Mana is the intended underlying resource where applicable.

Public direction

  • Most spells consume Mana.
  • Most normal spells are not limited by Long Rest uses.
  • Only especially powerful abilities commonly require rest-based limits.
  • Abilities may have individual cooldowns or conditions.

Design boundary

Aevum magic should not become a direct Baldur's Gate clone. Hidden ability lists, GM-only vaults and sealed mechanics remain out of public pages.
