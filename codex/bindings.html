Codex · Soul Bindings

What is bound is not born.

A binding is a connection imposed upon or formed with a soul. It is not an innate Trait, a learned Ability or a World Item.

Witnessed binding · state unresolved

Faaram's Soul Mark

Faaram placed a mark directly on Astera Zenith's soul. Before the later extraction incident, it limited part of her power, interfered with deeper perception and reacted to the severed Umbral Meridian current. It did not prevent the Hollow Star's complete integration.

Faaram subsequently took a major but incomplete portion of Astera's soul. The Mark's exact present state after that incident has not been confirmed. Its full purpose remains unknown.
