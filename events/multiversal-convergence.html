Convergence sequence

From fracture to continuity shift.

The Archive records the sequence without supplying unwitnessed physics.

  1. Initial Fractures

    Damage appeared across more than one universe. The fractures became points of contact rather than isolated wounds.

  2. Attraction

    The damaged universes began drawing together through the fractures.

  3. Convergence

    Two previously separate structures moved toward a shared state.

  4. Fusion Near Completion

    The old separation could no longer be treated as the stable continuity.

  5. Completion

    The convergence became complete. The resulting structure was one continuity, not two neighboring records.

  6. Timeline Shift

    Earlier records remained historically true to their own continuity but no longer described the whole current state.
