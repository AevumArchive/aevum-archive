Known ending

The previous road was brought to an end by the Aizen event. Its full truth remains sealed, but the opened shape is clear enough: a released force became the end of that path.

Remaining echoes

Older names such as OBAMA, Faaram, Raven, Tony and Jawohl remain in the archive because their stories still carry weight, even when their path is no longer active.

Opened sequence

  • Faaram once stood before the Overlord and received a rare mark remembered as Overlord's Grace.
  • That mark drew old attention toward the world and left the road more fragile than it seemed.
  • Aizen was released to strike Meruem, the Chimera Ant King.
  • The released blade did not remain a simple answer. The Aizen event became the ending of the road itself.
