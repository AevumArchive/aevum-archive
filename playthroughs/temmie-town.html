Eternal City — Temmie Town.

OBAMA, Faaram and Tony peacefully took responsibility for a village that would become an economic center, an anomaly magnet and eventually a city able to outlive its own timelines.

Founding

The rise of Temmie Town.

A peaceful change of leadership became the beginning of a city.

Growth under pressure

  • The first work centered on houses and civic infrastructure.
  • Armed raiders attacked regularly, forcing OBAMA, Tony and Faaram to defend the settlement with firearms.
  • The city's economic importance grew alongside its size.
  • A major Obamacare headquarters and another large Obamacare building employed many people.
  • Temmie Town repeatedly attracted events far stranger than an ordinary city should survive.

Anomaly record

The city that drew the impossible.

Rifts, creatures and curses found Temmie Town again and again.

The world rift

A tear appeared without a known cause. Vast numbers of demonic spiders poured through it and attacked the surrounding land. Skeletal horses and further anomalies appeared at other times.

The Sunflower

A gigantic mutated sunflower grew on Obamacare property. It was friendly toward OBAMA, yet powerful enough to destroy roughly 97% of Russia before Satoru Gojo exorcised it.

The publicly known account stated that a Sukuna finger was hidden within the plant. Gojo took the recovered cursed object after the exorcism.

Further record sealed

Hidden protector

Uwubama and the rewritten city.

The repeated return of Temmie Town was not a simple reset.

Reality in service of survival

  • Uwubama began as a clone of OBAMA and developed control over time, timelines, realities and the multiverse.
  • His central goal was that OBAMA and his friends should be safe.
  • When a timeline ended in catastrophe, a later playthrough could open with Temmie Town and its people restored.
  • The city sometimes continued to develop between these apparent endings.
  • Uwubama had altered reality behind the scenes.
Open Uwubama

Persistent location

Eternal City.

Temmie Town and its people were destroyed in multiple timelines, including one ended by the Chimera Ants. The city nevertheless returned. It now exists across servers and can continue developing outside an active playthrough.

No active player character currently lives there.

Uwubama's end

Almost erased.

The Time Gods intervened after too many violations of time, reality and multiverse. Uwubama was erased — almost completely. A fragment of his brain remains, leaving restoration theoretically possible.
