Origin city

From village to Alterra.

The earliest settlement became the core of a city built through administration, military technology and quiet protection.

The first walls

  • The original village core stood beside an early military sector protected by metal fencing and a stone wall.
  • The three players lived there while developing their first military systems.
  • New fortified districts grew around the old village, followed by large outer districts beyond the original defenses.
  • Alterra eventually possessed a highly advanced concealed protection bunker.

Player roles

Three foundations.

Each character shaped a different part of the city.

Sunstar 47

The organizational backbone of Alterra: administration, trade, outside relations and the systems that kept the city functioning.

Tony

Technology and military development. J.A.R.V.I.S. first emerged during this era.

Wade

An independent hitman who dealt with threats and Alterra's security from the background.

First nuclear catastrophe

The shelter John missed.

A major war produced the first great nuclear disaster of the early server history. John, one of the era's finest weapons developers, failed to reach the Vault and died from radiation.

Open John

Final incident

The Vault fruit breach.

The players removed far too many dangerous, carcinogenic fruits from a Vault and angered something within it. Creatures soon appeared in the Overworld, poisoning and corrupting everything around them.

End state: the contamination spreading from the Vault incident brought the first playthrough to an end.
