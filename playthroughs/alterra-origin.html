Oldest surviving chapter

The first recorded road.

This date is preserved as an archive working date, not an unquestionable historical timestamp.

Alterra stands at the beginning of the early recorded plugin era: the first known playthrough where the archive can still trace players, settlement growth, technical ambition and disaster into one coherent origin record.

Sunstar 47, Tony and Wade did not begin with a metropolis. They began with a comparatively small settlement and slowly transformed it into Alterra — a fast-developing technological city shaped by infrastructure, administration, trade, security, military experimentation and increasingly serious defensive construction.

The surviving record matters because it establishes a pattern that would echo through later Aevum history: ordinary places becoming major locations through player action, systems, conflict and memory.

City growth

The village that became a city.

Alterra expanded in layers rather than appearing fully formed.

01

Village

A small original settlement formed the earliest known core. Near it, the players established a controlled military tract.

02

Fortified core

Metal fencing, a smaller stone defensive wall and controlled military space protected the early operational area.

03

Inner expansion

The old village became increasingly important as development grew around it and new fortified sections formed behind fresh walls.

04

Outer Alterra

Eventually the city pushed beyond the older defenses, creating large developed areas outside the original fortifications.

Founders / Players

Three foundations.

Each player shaped a different side of Alterra's rise.

Administration

Sunstar 47

Sunstar 47 was effectively the organizational backbone of Alterra. His work covered administration, trade agreements, outside relations, city organization and the systems that kept the growing settlement functioning.

He represented the civic side of Alterra's rise: the part that turned expansion into something that could be coordinated, sustained and recognized.

Technology / Military

Tony

Tony focused heavily on technological development, military advancement, defensive systems and new infrastructure. One of the most important records from this era is the first early appearance of J.A.R.V.I.S.

This page does not fold later Vault 8 history into Alterra, but the connection is visible in hindsight.

Covert protection

Wade

Wade followed a more independent path. As a hitman, he was not primarily the public administrator or the visible builder of great systems.

His role was quieter: dealing with threats, protecting Alterra from the background and operating away from the city's visible political structure.

City at its height

A city in acceleration.

Alterra stopped feeling like three players upgrading a village. It became a city-state-like urban project with districts, defenses, trade, organization and technological infrastructure.

The archive does not preserve exact population figures. What it does preserve is the direction of growth: village → fortified settlement → city → large technological metropolis.

Military Alterra

The concealed protection bunker.

The military tract existed from very early in Alterra's history and remained important as the city expanded. Tony's work pushed the military and defensive side of the city forward.

Alterra eventually possessed a highly advanced concealed protection bunker — a protected fallback structure important for its era and a thematic ancestor to the much larger bunker and Vault projects that later shaped Tony's history.

J

Early artificial intelligence

The First J.A.R.V.I.S.

During the Alterra era, Tony developed J.A.R.V.I.S., an artificial intelligence that would remain connected to Tony's story far beyond this first playthrough. In hindsight, this is one of the most important technical origins preserved in the first record.

First nuclear catastrophe

The record turns dark.

Growth and technical optimism gave way to war, radiation, loss and survival.

The first great disaster

A major war occurred during the playthrough. The archive does not identify every side or cause, but it preserves the result: early server history experienced its first major nuclear catastrophe.

This event marks a clear break in the Alterra record. The world was no longer only expanding; it had become capable of large-scale ruin.

NPC memorial

John — Weapons Developer

John was one of the most important NPCs of the Alterra era, an exceptionally talented weapons developer regarded as one of the finest of his time.

When the nuclear catastrophe struck, John failed to reach the Vault or shelter in time. He died from radioactive exposure, becoming one of the earliest major named NPC deaths preserved by the archive.

Final chapter

The Vault Incident.

The end did not arrive as a clean single explosion.

The final collapse began when the players entered or interacted with a Vault-like location containing dangerous, carcinogenic fruits. They attempted to retrieve them — but took far too many.

Whatever watched over the Vault did not tolerate what had been taken. The surviving archive does not identify the force within the Vault, and this page does not pretend otherwise.

Afterward, strange creatures began appearing in the Overworld. They spread into the world, poisoned their surroundings and contaminated the environment until the situation spiraled beyond recovery.

Legacy

Why Alterra still matters.

Alterra is one of the earliest major player-built cities preserved by the archive. It introduced long-running themes: Tony's recurring technological history, the origin of J.A.R.V.I.S., protected bunker concepts, large-scale catastrophe and the transformation of ordinary settlements into locations with identity.

Canon update: Alterra carries Eternal Status. Its record did not end as a forgotten ruin; it remains one of the largest companies on the Desagment Plugin and one of the earliest examples of a player-made place surviving as a major institutional power.
