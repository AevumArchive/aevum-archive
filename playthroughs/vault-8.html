03

Underground city

Inside the mountain.

Vault 8 was never a narrow concrete shelter.

A city below ground

Full houses and buildings roughly eight or nine stories high stood within the mountain complex.

Military Wing

An enormous military sector connected to at least about forty nuclear silos and further defensive facilities.

D-Day

Tony's beach military base was linked to the wider system and produced ships among its other functions.

Fortification

  • Parts of the structure used materials comparable to Stonebricks Mark 40.
  • The shell was designed to resist extreme explosions, including an external nuclear strike.
  • Massively reinforced doors, security systems and military defenses made a conventional capture nearly impossible.
  • Wade helped in the early phase, then disappeared; Tony and J.A.R.V.I.S. continued the greater project alone.
04

Broken continuity

The destroyed timelines.

This playthrough did not receive one clean ending.

The Apex Predator assault

Tony killed the son of Cuban Bliss. The Apex Predators answered by attacking Vault 8, bombarding it with Titans and eventually detonating a nuclear weapon inside the complex. The Vault was destroyed.

Magnus Bane's hidden corrections

  • Tony should have died with the destroyed Vault.
  • Magnus Bane intervened, saved him and turned the timeline back.
  • Tony did not yet know that Magnus Bane was his father.
  • Later catastrophes drew further hidden interventions and corrected timelines.
  • Damaged and unplayable world states eventually ended the run in practice without producing one final canonical ending.

The Vault continues to exist in canon and can persist across servers, developing beyond an active playthrough. Finding it remains extraordinarily unlikely.

Tony's next return.

Repeated reincarnations and long separation from Magnus Bane have weakened Tony's former advantage. Another return after his latest death is possible, but no longer guaranteed.
